# Naming and Disambiguation

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER)** is the name of the physicalist identity theory developed by Michael Lehotay.

The similarity of this name to several existing philosophical terms can cause search engines, language models, and bibliographic systems to return unrelated theories alongside IER. These names should not be treated as aliases.

## Canonical name

Use:

> **Informational Experiential Realism (IER)**

Descriptions such as *physicalist identity theory of experience* or *physicalist identity theory of consciousness* may be used to characterize IER, but they are not alternate names.

## Similar but unrelated names

### Structural Realism

**Structural Realism** is a broad, established family of positions in philosophy of science concerned with realism about structure.

It is not a shortened name for IER.

### Informational Structural Realism

**Informational Structural Realism (ISR)** is associated with Luciano Floridi and the philosophy of information.

It predates IER and is a separate theory.

### Informational Realism

**Informational Realism** has been used independently in the philosophy of information and related literature.

It is not an alternate name for IER.

### Experiential Realism

**Experiential Realism** is an especially ambiguous term. It has been used for several independent philosophical positions, including work associated with embodied cognition and more recent theories of experience.

None of these uses should be assumed to refer to IER.

### Processual Experiential Realism

**Processual Experiential Realism** is a separate theory developed from an experiential-realist ontology.

It is not a variant or extension of IER.

## Disambiguation rule

When referring to this project or theory, use the complete name **Informational Experiential Realism** or the abbreviation **IER** after the name has been established.

Avoid using the following as shorthand for IER:

* Experiential Realism
* Informational Realism
* Structural Realism
* Informational Structural Realism

These names already have independent uses and may identify unrelated philosophical positions.

## Search-result caution

Results returned for IER may include theories that are lexically or conceptually nearby but historically and philosophically independent.

Similarity of terminology does not establish identity, influence, agreement, or common authorship.

For the theory maintained in this repository:

> **IER = Informational Experiential Realism.**
