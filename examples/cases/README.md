# Everyday Cases

Worked interpretations of familiar activities and social encounters through
Informational Experiential Realism (IER).

## Choose a case

- [Playing Fetch](playing-fetch.md): how a forgiving activity can survive failures of its immediate task.
- [Restaurant Underdetermination](restaurant-underdetermination.md): how one visible event can prompt different concerns and anticipated futures.

These non-canonical cases offer possible interpretations, not diagnoses of
people or animals or evidence of experiential boundaries. The
[core theory](../../IER/IER-model-overview.md) defines IER's criteria.
