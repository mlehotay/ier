# Malicious Compliance With Reality

> Free will is malicious compliance with reality.

This sounds like a joke.

Under Informational Experiential Realism, it captures something about freedom:
our activity can shape what happens while remaining entirely physical.

## The Shape of the Joke

“Malicious compliance” means:

* obeying rules exactly,
* while still bending outcomes from inside the rule system itself.

The maliciously compliant person does not:

* escape the rules,
* override the system,
* or violate the governing structure.

Instead:

* they exploit admissible maneuverability within the constraints.

The system remains lawful.

The person's activity helps shape the trajectory.

That shape turns out to resemble IER agency surprisingly well.

## Reality Still Wins

Under IER:

* nothing escapes physical law,
* no chooser stands outside causation,
* no metaphysical spontaneity interrupts physics,
* and no future is selected from beyond the world.

Reality always constrains continuation.

Gravity still works.
Momentum still matters.
Physical limits still apply.

The system cannot opt out.

## But Trajectories Still Matter

Learning guitar, correcting a steering movement, or responding to a demand can
change the physical conditions shaping what happens next. Under lawful dynamics:

* a correction can change the next movement,
* practice can change later capacities,
* the costs of sustaining an activity can change,
* and consequences can reshape later opportunities.

This means:

* systems can participate in forming their trajectories,
* change the conditions of later continuation,
* and sometimes make a previously available continuation unavailable.

All of this occurs through physical activity. A change in cost or ease does not
by itself mean that an alternative has become physically unreachable.

## Compliance Is Mandatory

A maliciously compliant employee does not ignore the handbook.

They obey it so literally that the organization discovers consequences it technically permitted all along.

Similarly:

* a Unified Experiential Field does not escape physics,
* its ongoing activity can change the conditions shaping its continuation,
* and it bears the relevant resolution and consequences from within.

Freedom therefore does not require:

* exemption from causation,
* indeterministic magic,
* or a hidden executive layer.

Under IER, diachronic freedom concerns:

> owned trajectory formation through ongoing physical activity and its consequences.

This presupposes a qualifying experiential operation. Feedback, flexible
responses, or deformable continuation geometry alone does not establish one.
The handbook analogy illustrates lawful participation; it supplies no test for
experience or agency.

## Degrees of Freedom

Physics accidentally gave matter degrees of freedom and now everyone has to deal with it.

That does not mean:

* anything can happen,
* every future is reachable,
* or law disappears.

Degrees of freedom describe ways a physical configuration can vary. They do
not, by themselves, tell us how many successors are physically admissible from
one fully specified present situation.

Physical determination means that the activity is exhaustively physical.
Whether the declared source and conditions admit one successor or several is
a further question. One successor actually occurring does not answer it.

Constraint remains absolute.

Our activity is among the physical processes through which continuation occurs.

## Continuous Compliance, Sometimes Foreclosure

A continuing experiential operation can adjust, sustain an activity, or gradually
relax without making a new choice at every moment. Ordinary continuation need
not contain collapse, and reversible shaping need not culminate in one.

Where sustainment fails and a connected region of admissible futures becomes
irreversibly unreachable, IER calls that foreclosure collapse. Collapse is the
loss of reachability itself. Historical incorporation and the propagation of
consequences have their own subsequent roles.

Choice proper requires the further conditions of owned collapse under live
multiplicity. Realizing a successor is insufficient to establish that event.

Freedom concerns the broader activity:

> the ongoing formation of a trajectory by a continuing operation that owns its resolution.

It includes reversible activity, irreversible resolution where it occurs, and
the bearing of inherited consequences. It is not confined to preparation for
a decision moment.

Or less formally:

> malicious compliance with reality.

## Compression

You cannot break reality’s rules.

The things you do are among reality's physical processes.

So the universe gets:

* lawful causation,
* intrinsic constraint,
* ongoing activity and its consequences,
* and somehow also:
  people arguing with spreadsheets,
  learning guitar,
  ruining their sleep schedules,
  inventing philosophy,
  and emotionally attaching themselves to tiny pixel cats.

Reality technically allowed all of this.
