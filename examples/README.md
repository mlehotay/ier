# Examples

Short illustrations and worked interpretations of Informational Experiential
Realism (IER), intended to build intuition for the
[core theory](../IER/IER-model-overview.md).

## Analogies

The [analogy collection](analogies/README.md) explores qualitative experience,
constraint, unity, and persistence through familiar physical systems.

## Everyday cases

- [Playing Fetch](cases/playing-fetch.md): how a forgiving activity can survive failures of its immediate task.
- [Restaurant Underdetermination](cases/restaurant-underdetermination.md): how one visible event can prompt different concerns and anticipated futures.

These non-canonical examples introduce no new theoretical criteria. The cases
offer possible interpretations; they do not diagnose people or animals or
establish experiential boundaries.
