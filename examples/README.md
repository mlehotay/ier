# Examples

Examples offer three ways into Informational Experiential Realism (IER):

- [Analogies](analogies/README.md): familiar physical systems illustrating qualitative differentiation, constraint, unity, and persistence.
- [Everyday cases](cases/README.md): worked interpretations of play and social encounters, focusing on continuation and differing expectations.
- [The Epistemic Kettle](collections/epistemic-kettle/README.md): behavioral evidence, intervention and rival explanations of an opaque temperature-control program.

The examples are non-canonical reading aids. For definitions and criteria,
read the [Specification](../IER/IER-specification.md).
