## Orientation

```text id="4x2m7q"

```

This assumption holds approximately within product future structures.

## Reachability Is Not Extendability

*IER reachability-margin* defines reachability as a binary boundary condition:

```text id="7m2r5w"
reachable
or
unreachable
```

A continuation is reachable if it remains admissible at the current history — future boundary.

However, reachability concerns only:

> immediate admissibility at the current frontier.

It does not guarantee:

* long-term continuation,
* stabilization,
* coherent propagation,
* or participation in any globally sustainable trajectory.

As *IER stabilization* states:

> “Immediate reachability does not guarantee continued existence of admissible successors.”

This distinction is foundational.


## Extendability

A continuation is globally extendable if:

> it belongs to at least one admissible trajectory that can continue coherently under the global admissible-future structure.

Very loosely:

```text id="6r4m1x"
reachable now
is weaker than
extendable through continuation
```

Global extendability therefore concerns:

* persistence of admissible successors,
* coherent continuation across time,
* compatibility with coupled future topology,
* and participation in stabilizable trajectories.

Extendability is not:

* preference,
* selection,
* success,
* optimization,
* or persistence of local coherence alone.

It is a structural property of admissible continuation geometry.


## Product and Non-Product Futures

In a product future structure:

```text id="5j1w8m"
local subsystem futures
combine freely
```

If subsystem A admits:

```text id="3q8v2m"
A1 A2
```

and subsystem B admits:

```text id="8n4r7x"
B1 B2
```

then all combinations remain globally admissible.

Local reachability therefore strongly predicts extendability.


In a non-product future structure:

```text id="1x7m3q"
some subsystem continuations
cannot coexist globally
```

Some combinations fail to belong to the admissible successor structure even while remaining locally coherent.

As established in *IER non-product-state-spaces*:

> a subsystem continuation may appear locally possible while remaining globally inadmissible.

This produces the central distinction:

```text id="7v2k5m"
local reachability
does not guarantee
global extendability
```


## Local Coherence Without Global Extension

Within non-product future structures, a continuation may remain:

* locally reachable,
* locally coherent,
* locally viable,
* and structurally describable,

while still failing to participate in any globally extendable trajectory.

The important point is subtle.

The continuation is not:

* corrected,
* rejected,
* overridden,
* or removed by external arbitration.

Rather:

```text id="5w8m2r"
the continuation
never belonged
to any globally extendable future structure
```

The apparent contradiction arises because local admissibility and global extendability are distinct structural conditions.


## Reachability Margin and Extendability

*IER reachability-margin* introduces directional reachability margin as a pre-collapse measure of proximity to foreclosure.

A continuation may therefore possess:

* positive reachability margin,
* local viability,
* and temporary coherence,

while still lacking globally extendable continuation.

This reveals an important structural separation:

| structure            | question                                                             |
| --- | --- |
| reachability         | can the continuation occur immediately?                              |
| reachability margin  | how close is it to foreclosure?                                      |
| viability            | how robust is the future region?                                     |
| stabilization        | can coherent propagation persist?                                    |
| global extendability | does the continuation belong to any globally sustainable trajectory? |

These layers must not be conflated.


## Extendability and Stabilization

*IER stabilization* defines stabilization in terms of extendable trajectories supporting coherent continuation under intrinsic constraint.

The stabilization set is $\Upsilon(A_{t^+})$.

A continuation may therefore remain:

* reachable,
* locally coherent,
* and temporarily viable,

while failing to belong to $\Upsilon(A_{t^+})$.

In such cases:

```text id="1m4k7x"
continuation exists locally
without globally stabilizable extension
```

This is not terminal collapse.

The global system may continue normally.

Only the local continuation fails to participate in any extendable admissible trajectory.


## Extendability and Settlement

*IER settlement* describes settlement as irreversible reduction of admissible futures under intrinsic constraint.

Within non-product future structures:

* settlement occurs globally,
* not independently per subsystem.

This means:

```text id="6x2m8w"
some locally coherent continuations
disappear
because no globally sustainable extension exists
```

No selector removes the continuation.

No observer chooses among futures.

The continuation simply fails to belong to the globally extendable admissible structure.

As *IER resolution* emphasizes:

> physical resolution does not require agency, selection, or ownership.

Extendability is therefore structural, not evaluative.

Ordinary branching-tree intuition assumes:

Non-product future structures invalidate this assumption.

```text id="2k7m5w"
topological non-extendability
```

## Why This Appears Paradoxical

Humans naturally reason locally.

We assume:

```text id="9r2m5v"
if each subsystem continuation appears possible
then the combined continuation should also remain possible
```

This inference fails in non-product future structures.

Global admissibility is not reconstructed from independent subsystem permissions.

Instead:

```text id="3x7m1q"
the global admissible structure
determines
which local continuations
can coexist coherently
```

The resulting phenomenon may appear like:

* contradiction,
* retroactive correction,
* remote influence,
* or delayed inconsistency.

Under the present framing, however, the issue is simpler:

> some locally coherent continuations were never globally extendable.
