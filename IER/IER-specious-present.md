# Specious Present

## Coherent Operation and the Appearance of a Thick Present

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.6)**\
*T2 · Case · Explanatory · Non-Normative*

## Abstract

The canonical specious present is a nonzero-duration coherence window required for qualifying UEF operation. This article separately proposes that current co-determining organization can contribute to the appearance of a thick present. Locally reversible integration envelopes, salience, inherited history and ongoing succession have distinct roles; a local collapse need not end all present coherence. Felt thickness supplies neither a measured UEF threshold nor a proof of token continuity.

## Status, Scope, and Authority

Named full-corpus owners fix the technical meanings used here. [Theory](IER-theory.md) provides the public integrated account; this article states the premises needed for its own explanation.

[Continuity](IER-continuity.md) fixes the canonical specious-present meaning. It concerns physically coherent operation across nonzero duration, without assigning a universal measured minimum. This article's phenomenological proposal is additional explanatory work, rather than a replacement definition.

*Time* (`IER-time.md`) describes operation-relative temporal asymmetry. Clock-indexed physical description and physically implemented buffering or timing are permitted. They are not a container holding earlier experience or a sufficient subject criterion.

## Orientation — Why the Present Seems Thick

A phrase can appear as more than isolated notes; a word as more than disconnected syllables; a gesture as one organized movement. These questions motivate an account of current organization across an interval. They do not establish that a completed past experience is literally retained inside a moving present.

The relevant question is how earlier physical effects and current participation remain jointly effective in continuing operation. That question allows ordinary physical mechanisms while resisting a second temporal object that would itself require an experiencer.

## Two Different Claims

First, qualifying operation requires a nonzero coherence window. This is the constitutive use of “specious present.” Repeated coherent windows alone do not establish numerical persistence: all five stage-continuity conditions must connect them.

Second, this article proposes that actively co-determining organization may contribute to apparent present thickness within an independently qualifying UEF. Its extent is relative to a specified organization and comparison, not the maximal duration of a subject or a clock-free universal quantity. The proposal supplies no completed mapping from physical extent to felt duration.

## Reversible Co-Determination and Integration Envelopes

*Integration Envelopes* (`IER-integration-envelopes.md`) describes recoverability of a locally reversible organization before its relaxation or foreclosure. Current participation, binding, soft bonding and curvature can sustain related structure while the relevant alternatives remain reachable.

An earlier syllable can still condition the organization of a word without an earlier experience being replayed. A current record or buffer can physically support that organization, but neither its presence nor its absence establishes qualifying operation. *Memory* (`IER-memory.md`) concerns present episodic re-entry; effective history is broader than recall.

Envelope loss concerns the specified recoverable organization. It does not prove that every causal effect elsewhere has vanished, that all memory is impossible, or that the UEF token ended.

## Salience and Joint Recruitment

*Salience* (`IER-salience.md`) describes graded recruitment organization. Stronger recruitment may help related elements remain jointly consequential, or may disrupt their organization. Neither direction is a universal law of felt thickness.

A melody example makes the comparison concrete. Suppose earlier-note effects remain physically effective while current notes recruit one organized phrase. That supports a co-determination description. If attention changes and the phrase organization disperses, that local organization may cease to be recoverable even though records and other historical effects remain. The listener's numerical identity must be assessed separately.

## Relaxation, Collapse and Event Boundaries

A particular reversible organization can end through relaxation, reconfiguration or foreclosure under the respective owners. Collapse is atomic irreversible support loss at a physically justified comparison. It does not imply that all organization in the UEF loses coherence or that the entire present ends.

[Event Segmentation](IER-event-segmentation.md) separately proposes how propagated, cognitively registered consequences can articulate a collapse-associated event boundary. A perceptual or narrative boundary can also be described without establishing that physical foreclosure occurred. Event segmentation is therefore not a stopwatch for constitutive windows.

## Nested Scales and External Scaffolding

Motor, auditory, linguistic and narrative organization may sustain different overlapping spans of coordination. These spans do not automatically identify several experiential subjects or a single universal temporal window. Their physical description, cognitive role and qualitative comparison retain distinct burdens.

Musical phrasing, conversation and ritual can physically influence recruitment, timing and continuation. They are neither an extra collapse operator nor categorically causally powerless. Whether an intervention changes successor membership, costs or local coherence requires the appropriate comparison.

## Empirical Bridges and Limits

Temporal integration and grouping research can motivate tests of the co-determination proposal. A neural timescale or reported duration alone establishes neither the canonical coherence relation nor its phenomenal correspondence. No empirical identity is admitted here.

## Summary

Nonzero-duration coherence is a constitutive requirement; apparent thickness is an explanatory target. Current co-determination offers a conditional bridge between them. Neither local envelope duration, a felt present nor event segmentation replaces full physical qualification and produced-and-inherited token continuity.
