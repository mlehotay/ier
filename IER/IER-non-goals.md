# IER Non-Goals

## What the Theory Is Not Trying to Do

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.12.0)**\
*T3 · Constraint · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article states the durable non-goals of Informational Experiential Realism.

It introduces no new ontological primitive, identity condition, mechanism, diagnostic, threshold, empirical attribution, or ethical rule. It does not turn a present limitation into a permanent prohibition merely because the work has not yet been done.

The IER Specification remains authoritative. The précis, quick start, explanandum, scope, critics, and a priori articles control the positive proposal and its established limits. If this article conflicts with those sources, the higher-authority claim prevails.

The purpose of a non-goal is narrower:

> to identify a demand that IER need not satisfy in order to succeed as the theory it is.

A non-goal is not evidence that the demand is meaningless, that no other theory should pursue it, or that the associated inquiry lacks value.

## The Positive Project

IER is a physicalist identity proposal about experience.

It proposes that one qualifying, temporally continuing physical operation is numerically identical with:

- one total experience-as-process; and
- one experiential subject.

IER aims to characterize the unity, ownership boundary, constitutive participation, qualitative differentiation, continuation, and cessation of that operation without adding a phenomenal product, inner bearer, semantic substance, or second causal pathway.

Its positive case is abductive and a posteriori. The physical regime can be specified without using experience as a physical input, but physical specifiability does not deductively prove the identity claim.

The non-goals below preserve that project’s form and boundary.

## IER Will Not Derive Physicalism for a Non-Physicalist

IER begins within physicalism. It does not attempt to compel someone who rejects physicalism, physical causal completeness, concrete process identity, or identity theory as a legitimate metaphysical form.

IER can:

- state its physicalist commitments clearly;
- compare their costs with dualism, idealism, panpsychism, functionalism, and other alternatives;
- answer objections internal to its proposal;
- show that its account is coherent, physically specifiable, and explanatorily economical; and
- expose when an objection simply presupposes a rival ontology.

IER will not derive physicalism from premises acceptable to every possible critic. Nor will it add nonphysical properties, phenomenal primitives, or an ontological bridge solely to secure agreement from someone who denies its starting point.

A critic can understand IER, test its consistency, challenge its physical adequacy, or reject its abductive case without accepting physicalism. What IER does not promise is metaphysically neutral persuasion.

## IER Will Not Deductively Prove the Identity Claim

IER does not claim that a physical description logically entails experience for every reader independently of background commitments.

It will not:

- define a UEF as whatever is conscious and call the identity proved;
- derive phenomenality from purely non-experiential vocabulary by formal deduction;
- treat structural resemblance as numerical identity;
- treat successful physical modeling as proof of consciousness; or
- claim that coherence makes the theory unavoidable.

The identity proposal remains defeasible. Its support comes from the fit between total experience and a qualifying operation across unity, differentiation, boundary, causal role, counterfactual organization, temporal extension, continuity, and cessation, together with the absence of an independently varying phenomenal bearer or product.

## IER Will Not Produce a Quale Codebook

IER does not aim to map physical structures one-to-one onto externally named qualia such as *red*, *wet*, *painful*, *sweet*, or *minty*.

On the identity proposal, the complete qualifying physical organization is not a code that produces, represents, or points to an additional qualitative item. It is the qualitative experience. Requiring a second fact that explains why the organization yields a particular quale would deny the identity and restart the bridge regress.

This non-goal does not prohibit descriptive research. Physical organization, reports, discriminations, learned categories, stimulus conditions, and phenomenological descriptions may be compared or mapped for scientific and explanatory purposes. Such mappings are:

- relational and vocabulary-dependent;
- limited by first-person and third-person access;
- descriptions of correspondences among organizations; and
- not an exhaustive translation from physical structure into an independently existing phenomenal alphabet.

IER therefore permits local and useful mappings. It does not promise a universal lookup table from structure to quale.

## IER Will Not Become a General Consciousness Detector

IER distinguishes ontological constitution from epistemic access.

It does not aim to provide:

- a behavioral checklist for consciousness;
- a neural, computational, informational, or architectural shortcut;
- a scalar consciousness score;
- a probability of subjecthood derived from functional similarity;
- certification of unfamiliar biological or artificial systems;
- an inventory of every subject in the universe; or
- an empirical boundary map for every organism.

Concrete attribution may remain underdetermined even when the theory states what would constitute a UEF. Failure to identify the UEF boundary in an arbitrary system does not make the ownership boundary undefined, and a proposed measurement does not acquire criterial authority merely because it is useful.

## IER Will Not Identify the Subject with a Familiar Object by Default

IER does not aim to vindicate an antecedently preferred boundary such as:

- the organism;
- the brain;
- the nervous system;
- the skin;
- a cognitive workspace;
- a self-model;
- a functional module; or
- the largest causally connected network.

The subject boundary is structural and regime-relative. Organismic processes may support, condition, perturb, or sustain a UEF while remaining outside its owned operation. A process belongs constitutively only when it participates bilaterally in the same present organization under intrinsic constraint, whose physical criterion remains incomplete.

The theory is not required to make the subject coincide with ordinary biological, anatomical, computational, or social units.

## IER Will Not Make Cognition the Basis of Experience

IER does not aim to explain experience by reducing it to:

- intelligence;
- reasoning;
- representation;
- reportability;
- access consciousness;
- language;
- memory;
- self-modeling;
- metacognition; or
- behavioral flexibility.

These organizations may occur within a UEF and may change what the experience is like. They do not define whether experience exists. Noncognitive physical processes can participate constitutively when they belong to the same owned operation.

IER will not restore cognition as a hidden threshold after explicitly rejecting it as the basis of subjecthood.

## IER Will Not Treat Abstract Function or Computation as Sufficient

IER does not aim to identify experience at the level of an abstract program, input-output profile, functional role, simulation, graph isomorphism, or substrate-neutral description alone.

Descriptions can be multiply realized. Numerical identity belongs to the concrete token operation with its actual causal-modal organization, history, boundary, and continuation. A model of a UEF is not thereby a UEF, and a simulation of intrinsic constraint does not establish that the simulating process bears the modeled constraint as its own.

This does not make computation irrelevant. Computational organization may participate physically in a UEF. What IER refuses is sufficiency by abstract equivalence alone.

## IER Will Not Add an Inner Observer or Phenomenal Product

IER does not aim to explain who has an experience by placing another bearer behind it.

It will not posit:

- a self-substance inside the UEF;
- a witness that receives the field;
- an internal screen or audience;
- a phenomenal object generated for a subject;
- a controller that owns the operation from outside it; or
- a second mental causal stream accompanying physical dynamics.

The UEF operation is the total experience and subject. Ownership names the non-externalizable organization of its frontier, not a further owner.

## IER Will Not Grant Experience Epistemic Authority

IER does not aim to make experience transparent, infallible, incorrigible, or self-certifying.

Experience does not by itself guarantee:

- true belief;
- accurate introspection;
- correct self-attribution;
- correct identification of causes;
- access to its physical implementation;
- knowledge of another subject; or
- knowledge that IER is true.

Mineness and for-me-ness concern ownership of constraint, not privileged access to mind-independent reality. A subject can be fully experiential and profoundly mistaken.

## IER Will Not Explain Why Experience or This Subject Exists at All

IER aims to say what experience is and what makes one continuing physical operation one subject. It does not aim to supply a cosmic, teleological, moral, or experiential reason why:

- experience exists anywhere;
- this universe contains UEFs;
- a particular subject exists;
- experience occurs here rather than elsewhere; or
- this life began at this time.

Those are questions of physical and biological history, contingency, or broader metaphysics. IER does not treat experience as selecting, summoning, or justifying its own existence.

## IER Will Not Reproduce Every Folk-Psychological Category

IER does not aim to preserve every ordinary distinction among faculties, mental objects, inner resources, representations, selves, and contents as a separate ontological part.

Ordinary and scientific vocabularies may remain useful at their own explanatory scales. IER may translate them into patterns of participation, constraint, topology, history, access, and interpretation without claiming that each familiar noun names an independent component of experience.

Failure to preserve a folk category as an ontological primitive is not elimination of the phenomenon it helps describe.

## IER Will Not Turn Its Analogies into Evidence or Implementations

The Stapled Net, Billowed Sail, Hello World systems, fictional cases, and other examples clarify selected relations. They do not establish:

- experience;
- intrinsic ownership;
- bilateral constitutive participation;
- UEF formation;
- numerical subject identity; or
- an engineering recipe.

IER does not aim to win assent by the vividness of an analogy. Illustrative fit is subordinate to the physical and metaphysical account.

## IER Will Not Derive Moral Rank from Cognitive or Functional Rank

IER does not aim to rank experiential subjects by intelligence, language, productivity, social recognition, computational power, or behavioral sophistication.

Subjecthood and experiential standing are categorical at the regime level. Participation, content, capability, vulnerability, and practical uncertainty may vary without turning subjecthood into a scalar achievement.

IER also does not aim to derive complete public policy from ontology alone. Ethical and precautionary reasoning requires further facts and normative commitments.

## IER Will Not Be a Complete Theory of Everything Mental

IER is a theory of experience and experiential subjecthood. It is not by itself a complete theory of:

- perception;
- thought;
- language;
- intelligence;
- rationality;
- personality;
- psychopathology;
- social organization;
- culture;
- morality; or
- all biological regulation.

IER can constrain accounts of these phenomena where they occur within or couple to experience. It need not replace the sciences and theories that explain them at other levels.

## Non-Goals Are Not Exemptions from Theoretical Work

The non-goal boundary must not become a shelter for avoidable weakness.

IER still owes:

- a coherent and non-circular statement of its identity candidate;
- sufficiently precise individuation, participation, ownership, and continuity conditions;
- compatibility with established physical evidence;
- clear separation of constitutive processes from external conditioning and support;
- consistency across its canonical formulations;
- serious comparison with competing explanations;
- explicit treatment of counterexamples and failure cases; and
- an honest, defeasible abductive case for preferring identity.

Formalization, physical modeling, biological application, and empirical comparison remain legitimate development goals when they sharpen or test the proposal. They are not consciousness diagnostics merely by existing, and their present incompleteness cannot be relabeled a non-goal.

The distinction is:

| Non-goal | Continuing burden |
| --- | --- |
| Persuade from metaphysically neutral premises | State assumptions and compare explanatory costs honestly |
| Deductively prove experience from physics | Make the abductive identity case as strong and defeasible as possible |
| Produce a universal quale codebook | Explain qualitative identity clearly and permit disciplined local comparison |
| Certify every candidate subject | Define constitution and individuation without circularity |
| Make the UEF coincide with the organism | Explain the ownership boundary and bilateral membership relation |
| Reduce experience to cognition or function | Explain their organization within or outside the UEF |
| Replace every neighboring science | Remain physically compatible and integrate relevant findings |

## How the Non-Goal Boundary Can Change

These non-goals are durable consequences of IER’s present identity-theoretic form, not immutable declarations about all possible successor theories.

A non-goal should change only when:

- the positive project changes;
- a supposed non-goal is shown to be required for internal coherence;
- a stronger result becomes available without contradicting the identity claim; or
- the canonical authority structure explicitly revises the boundary.

Ordinary progress, new applications, or improved evidence need not expand the explanandum.

## Final Compression

IER will not:

- convert a non-physicalist by abandoning physicalism;
- deductively manufacture experience from a description;
- translate physical structures into a universal list of qualia;
- certify every conscious system;
- assume that organism, brain, cognition, or self-model fixes the subject;
- substitute function, computation, behavior, or intelligence for concrete identity;
- add an observer behind experience;
- grant experience epistemic authority;
- explain the cosmic purpose of subjecthood;
- turn analogies into evidence; or
- become a complete theory of everything mental, moral, biological, or social.

IER aims to identify and characterize the concrete continuing physical operation that is one total experience and one subject.

Its non-goals keep that claim exact. They do not relieve the theory of making it coherent, physically substantive, and abductively worth accepting.
