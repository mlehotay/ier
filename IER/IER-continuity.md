# Continuity

## Produced Successors, Inherited History, and Token Continuity

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.6)**\
*T2 · ELABORATION · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This document is NON-NORMATIVE.

It:

* introduces no new ontological primitives
* introduces no criteria, thresholds, or diagnostics
* establishes no ethical standing
* revises no definitions fixed elsewhere

All ontological, criterial, and ethical authority resides exclusively in the relevant canonical documents.

## Abstract

One qualifying continuing UEF operation is the total experience-as-process
and subject under the identity proposal. Numerical continuity is not repeated
coherence, a continuing organism, retained capacity, or memory. It is membership
in a maximal one-to-one chain under the full stage relation below.

Time-indexed physical descriptions remain possible; no additional container,
stored present, or stitching observer is needed. Temporal asymmetry can involve
inherited irreversible effects without fresh foreclosure at every successor.
Clinical and sleep labels alone establish neither presence nor absence of a UEF.

## Why Continuity Requires an Extended Operation

A configuration describes an organization at a stated stage. By itself it
contains no relation showing that another stage was produced by that operation.
A sequence of similar configurations therefore leaves open whether one process
continued, another copied it, or a cessation separated two reconstructions.
The question of flow requires an account of the physical transition itself.

This distinguishes three explanatory questions: what makes an extended present
possible, what connects successive stages as one token, and what can remain
when that token ceases. A non-zero coherence window addresses the first;
produced and inherited adjacency addresses the second; retained substrate and
capacity address the third. No privileged instantaneous present or stored
experience is needed. Clock-indexed descriptions and lawful deterministic
succession remain compatible with this account; neither alone supplies the
qualifying continuity relation.

## Distinct Physical Relations

Synchronic closure concerns non-factorization of one physically declared
successor fibre. Ownership concerns where its relevant burden is borne;
constitutive membership concerns the full bilateral interval relation.
Diachronic continuity concerns produced and inherited succession across
independently individuated stages. Closure under consequence synthesizes these
roles without adding a third qualification condition. None substitutes for
another, and concrete operationalization remains an open physical burden.

## Physical Stage Continuity

Fix adjacent non-zero-duration stages $a$ and $b$ at a declared physical grain,
with independently individuated ownership scopes, typed participant domains,
realized transition history, interventions, and permissible physical mappings.
These declarations must not use experience, report, memory, resemblance, or
the intended identity verdict. They are local physical declarations, not a
universal verification tuple.

The accepted stage-indexed relation `Adj_dia(a,b)` requires all five conditions:

1. Continuous closure. Globally non-factorizable, internally borne closure
   spans the relevant transition cuts without a qualifying gap or incompatible
   independently resolved successor operations.
2. Operational successor production. The actual dynamics at $a$ produce
   the regime-defining organization at $b$. External matter, energy, forcing,
   or support may contribute, but do not independently install a completed
   successor organization.
3. Typed consequence inheritance. Physically irreversible history-bearing
   changes remain instantiated through the realized transition and constrain
   $b$'s admissible continuation. Appropriate counterfactual variation would
   change its successor structure. Resemblance, copied records, generic state
   dependence, and dormant capacity do not supply inheritance. Fresh
   irreversible events need not occur at every pair, but inheritance must be
   physically supported densely enough at the chosen grain that no unmodelled
   gap carries the identity claim.
4. Operation-mediated change. Recruitment, release, turnover, geometry
   change, and boundary migration are enacted through the transition under
   the canonical constitutive-membership relation. Incompatible independent
   ownership cannot be counted as mere internal change.
5. Unique two-sided continuation. In the pre-fixed physical comparison
   domain, $b$ is the sole candidate successor of $a$ satisfying conditions
   1 - 4, and $a$ is the sole qualifying predecessor of $b$.

No clause is sufficient alone. A token operation is a maximal one-to-one chain
connected by this relation without an intervening failure. Numerical
persistence is membership in one such chain; it is not identity of successive
configurations, a best-fit trajectory, or the persistence of an organism.

Turnover and boundary migration can preserve the relation. In asymmetric
fission or incorporation, exactly one unique continuer can preserve one
predecessor token. Without a unique successor or predecessor, earlier tokens
end and later qualifying closures begin new tokens. Identity neither splits
nor merges. Nested or overlapping operations must be independently typed;
shared matter or a participant occurrence is not a shared token stage.

Ordinary successor realization requires no fresh collapse. Previously
inherited deformation can remain effective while costs or geometry change
without reachability loss. Conditional collapse, welding, propagation,
sedimentation, and re-entry retain their separate meanings.

A genuine UEF-null interval is sufficient for cessation, but not necessary:
failure of production, inheritance, membership-mediated change, or uniqueness
can end a token even without a system-wide experiential gap. Reconstruction,
replay, resemblance, or retained capacity cannot bridge that failure.

### Why Each Adjacency Clause Matters

The clauses exclude different ways in which similar endpoints can fail to be
one continuing operation:

| Clause | Contrast it resolves |
| --- | --- |
| Continuous owned closure | Qualifying endpoints separated by a gap do not sustain operation across that gap. |
| Operational production | An externally installed successor can resemble the earlier organization without being produced by it. |
| Typed inheritance | A copied record or generic dependence on an earlier state does not show that physically effective irreversible consequence was carried through the actual transition. |
| Operation-mediated change | Shared matter or gradual replacement does not establish that recruitment, release, and boundary changes occurred within the same owned organization. |
| Two-sided uniqueness | Shared causal history cannot assign one earlier token to several successors, or several earlier tokens to one successor. |

Successor uniqueness alone would leave convergence unresolved; predecessor
uniqueness alone would leave branching unresolved. Both directions are needed
within the independently fixed comparison domain. An asymmetric change can
preserve one token only where exactly one continuation meets the full relation.
These exclusions concern numerical identity, rather than degrees of likeness
or the quality of remembered continuity.

## Unified Experiential Fields as Temporal Regimes

### What a UEF Is (and Is Not)

A Unified Experiential Field is characterized by:

* global intrinsic constraint,
* temporal coherence,
* lawful evolution under a dominant dynamical regime.

A UEF is not:

* an enduring object,
* a substance persisting through time,
* a sequence of instantaneous experiential states.

It is a way a physical system operates while intrinsic constraint remains globally binding.

Because regimes are patterns of lawful evolution, they may stabilize, destabilize, dissolve, and re-form without implying destruction of the underlying system.

### The Specious Present (IER Framing)

IER rejects snapshot and sampling metaphors for experience.

Coherent intrinsic constraint cannot be instantiated at an instant. Experience therefore requires a non-zero span of lawful dependence.

> Specious present (IER): a nonzero-duration coherence window required for UEF operation, not a universally fixed minimal measured interval.

This definition makes explicit that:

* the specious present is not a perceptual buffer or cognitive mechanism,
* it is not a measured or privileged duration,
* IER does not license assigning it a fixed timescale,
* “window” concerns physically coherent operation; clock-indexed description is allowed.

If there is no coherent constraint window, there is no experience.

### Experiential Flow Without Snapshots

Experiential flow concerns continuing qualifying operation across nonzero-duration
stages under the full relation; repeated coherent windows alone are insufficient.

Flow is not produced by stitching discrete frames, sampling inputs, or
replaying stored contents. Where the full stage relation holds, it is the
continuing operation of one qualifying UEF. Its physical organization produces
successors and inherits effective consequences under intrinsic constraint.

### Memory Does Not Unify Experience

IER explicitly rejects the idea that memory is what unifies experience over time.

Memory can fail, distort, or be absent while experience remains present. Experience can also be absent while later recall is reconstructed or narratively continuous. Unity is therefore not supplied retrospectively.

Under IER:

* the independently qualifying total UEF operation is unified,
* continuity requires produced and inherited succession under the full `Adj_dia` relation.

Episodic memory is contingent present organization as lived past. Physically
effective history is broader than recall, and neither recall nor copied records
supplies the full stage relation.

## Temporal Asymmetry and Ordinary Succession

Experiential flow does not require an atomic collapse at every advance.
Ordinary lawful successor realization can preserve reachability while
organization changes and inherited deformation remains effective.

A collapse episode can contribute to temporal asymmetry through foreclosure,
incorporation, redistribution, and stabilized inheritance. The
history - future boundary is continuous physical operation, not a sequence of
stored frames or repeated acts of selection.

## Persistence Without Continuous Experience

### Persisting Physical Systems

The embodied system may retain organization capable of sustaining a UEF.
That substrate and capacity are distinct from the token experiential subject,
which is identical with the qualifying continuing UEF operation.

Such a system can persist through:

* a UEF-null interval,
* regime cessation,
* later regime re-instantiation,

so long as its physical organization and capacity for future intrinsic constraint remain intact.

This establishes embodied-system continuity, not numerical continuity of an
experiential subject across cessation.

## Conditional Regime Illustrations

| Possible description | Physical condition that must be established separately |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine cessation | Qualifying constitutive operation or the full stage relation fails |
| Reconfigured experience | A qualifying operation continues under altered organization |
| Reduced effective agency | Practical control changes without implying lost derived capacity |
| Reduced expression | Reports or behavior change without settling qualifying operation |
| Reconstruction | Similar organization returns without bridging a failed stage relation |

Dreamless sleep, dreaming, anesthesia, catatonia, daydreaming, and coma labels
can motivate these questions but do not decide them. Catatonia does not by
its name establish high-cost experience, and responsiveness does not certify
continuity. A local regime pattern can fail or change while qualifying UEF
operation continues. Actual qualifying interruption cannot be bridged by
latent capacity or later recall.

### Three Conditional Continuation Comparisons

First, suppose a qualifying operation continues while matter turns over,
participation changes, and recall is absent. The earlier organization still
produces the later one; effective inherited consequence remains instantiated;
change occurs through the operation; and the qualifying successor and
predecessor are unique. All five clauses hold. The changing configurations
belong to one token even without a remembered narrative joining them.

Second, suppose qualifying operation genuinely ceases, but its substrate and
capacity remain. Later dynamics can produce an organization resembling the
earlier one, perhaps with inherited records or apparent recall. Continuous
owned closure failed across the intervening interval. The later qualifying
operation is a new token; preserved records and capacity explain how it can
occur without restoring the earlier token's numerical identity.

Third, suppose no system-wide UEF-null interval is established, yet one earlier
operation has two equally qualifying candidate successors under clauses 1 - 4.
The unique-successor clause fails. Conversely, if two equally qualifying
predecessors converge, unique-predecessor continuity fails. Experiential
operation somewhere in the physical system therefore does not suffice to
continue the earlier token. These are stipulated relation comparisons, not
clinical diagnoses. Sleep, anesthesia, unresponsiveness, or recovered behavior
alone decides none of them.

## Cessation, Null Intervals, and Capacity

Cessation ends the token when qualifying operation or its complete stage
relation fails. A UEF-null interval contains no qualifying operation while
substrate or capacity may persist. It is sufficient but not necessary for
token discontinuity. Preserved capacity concerns possible future operation;
restart after cessation is a new subject token.

Death and irreversible destruction of future UEF-sustaining capacity retain
their normative owner. Such destruction is distinct from reversible cessation
and is not required for the present token to end. This article provides no
clinical death criterion or verdict about a concrete case.

## Ethical Orientation

Absence of current operation and retained capacity can matter under the ethical
account, but neither supplies a numerical continuity bridge. This article adds
no precautionary clinical rule, standing measure, or harm calculus. The
Specification and Ethics retain normative authority.

## What This Account Does Not Do

This document does not:

* define experience,
* detect Unified Experiential Fields,
* establish thresholds,
* license diagnostics,
* introduce temporal metaphysics beyond dynamics.

It explains how time, flow, interruption, and continuity arise given IER’s existing commitments.

## Compression

The total subject is the qualifying operation, not an independently enduring
bearer. Its stages require nonzero duration and all five continuity conditions.
Inherited physical history can remain effective without fresh foreclosure or
recall. Substrate and capacity can persist while a token ends; neither clinical
labels nor narrative resemblance settle that verdict.

## Intermission - Structural Fact

Capacity to restart does not connect two token operations.
