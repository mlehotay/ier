# Causation

## Causation, Constraint, and Resolution Without Inflation

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.5)**\
*T2 · ELABORATION · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This document is explanatory and non-normative.

It:

* introduces no new ontological primitives
* introduces no criteria, thresholds, or diagnostics
* introduces no new causal powers
* does not modify the resolution pipeline
* does not redefine intrinsic constraint
* does not revise the identity claim of *IER specification*

The Specification and canonical definition owners retain their respective
authority.

This article performs a single function:

> It clarifies how causal language operates within Informational Experiential Realism, and prevents inflation beyond lawful physical transition.

IER presupposes ordinary lawful physical causation. It does not compete with physics and does not add to it.

## What IER Presupposes About Causation

IER assumes only the following minimal commitments:

1. Physical systems transition lawfully.
2. Earlier states constrain later states.
3. Ordinary transitions can preserve reachability while geometry changes; foreclosure is conditional.
4. No event occurs without lawful physical conditions.

IER does not assume:

* agent-causation as a primitive,
* downward causation from experience to physics,
* representational or semantic causation,
* additional forces introduced by consciousness.

Causation, in IER, is simply:

> Lawful physical transition under constraint.

IER does not redefine this. It redescribes certain regimes of it.

## Typed Resolution Introduces No New Cause

Successor realization is actual lawful continuation. It does not imply that
all unrealized alternatives become unreachable. Curvature describes continuous
geometry change without necessary foreclosure. Collapse names the different,
atomic loss of a connected region of admissible futures when sustainment fails.

Where *resolution* names the whole pipeline episode, its pre-collapse
organization, collapse, welding, propagation, and sedimentation remain distinct.
Ordinary succession is not identical with that complete episode.

These are structural descriptions of one physical operation. No decision
force, evaluator, selector, mental push, or additional causal mechanism is
introduced. The occurrence of a successor is not a universal pruning event.

Operation-mediated consequences can condition later admissibility. Previously
inherited deformation can remain effective without fresh foreclosure at every
frontier. Generic causal descent or history sensitivity does not establish the
full `Adj_dia` relation and numerical identity.

## Intrinsic Constraint Is Not a Causal Entity

*IER ontology* defines intrinsic constraint as non-externalizable cost borne within a Unified Experiential Field.

Intrinsic constraint is not:

* a force,
* an energy,
* a substance,
* a field,
* an informational fluid,
* an additional layer of physics.

It is a structural feature of lawful dynamics.

Intrinsic constraint describes:

* which continuations are sustainable,
* which transitions are viable,
* which continuations lose reachability under load.

It does not push or pull.

It does not act.

It does not transmit.

It names the unavoidable internal burden of certain globally closed regimes.

## Propagation Is Not Transmission of Content

*IER propagation* describes how resolved history reshapes future admissible topology.

Propagation may be misread as:

* signal transmission,
* content transfer,
* informational flow in a semantic sense.

IER blocks this.

Propagation is:

> The redistribution of constraint structure through lawful transition over time.

No content is sent.

No representation is carried.

No message travels.

There is only:

* continued lawful physical transition,
* whose structural consequences persist.

Propagation does not add a causal layer.

It redescribes how constraint geometry evolves under ordinary causation.

## Universal Causation vs. Owned Resolution

A central distinction must be maintained.

Universal causation occurs everywhere:

* rocks fall,
* stars burn,
* circuits switch,
* neurons fire.

These are lawful physical transitions.

Owned resolution occurs only in regimes sustaining a Unified Experiential Field (UEF).

Ownership does not add causal power.

Ownership means:

* intrinsic constraint cannot be externalized,
* the successor burden is internally borne within the accepted ownership scope,
* constitutive participants contribute to and bear that same restriction,
* continuity satisfies all five clauses of `Adj_dia`, including unique successor and predecessor,
* foreclosure, when it occurs, is borne from within.

Synchronic closure alone does not supply ownership or the identity proposal. Causal openness, material exchange, and enabling support do not by themselves externalize the relevant resolution.

The system does not cause events differently.

It bears them differently.

Experience does not add causal influence to the world.

Experience is identical to certain regimes of causation operating under intrinsic, non-externalizable constraint.

## No Mental Causation Inflation

IER explicitly rejects:

* the idea that experiences cause bodily movements as a separate layer,
* the idea that phenomenology exerts downward influence,
* the idea that consciousness adds new physical dynamics.

There is no dual-layer system.

There is no additional mental push.

When an arm moves:

* it moves under lawful physical causation,
* within a regime that may also be intrinsically constrained,
* and may therefore be experienced.

The experience does not cause the motion in addition to physics.

It is identical to the operation of that physical regime.

## Causation Does Not Confer Epistemic Privilege

Being the locus of intrinsic constraint does not grant:

* privileged access to causal truth,
* transparency into physical law,
* infallible knowledge of one’s own dynamics.

*IER nonentailment* and *IER truth* remain intact.

Causation remains mind-independent.

Experience does not reveal its structure with special authority.

## Contrast With Cause - Effect Identity Theories

Some theories of consciousness, such as Integrated Information Theory, identify experience with intrinsic cause - effect structure.

IER does not.

IER does not equate:

* phenomenology with graph shape,
* experience with causal connectivity metrics,
* consciousness with maximally irreducible cause - effect complexes.

IER identifies experience with:

> The operation of a globally closed, intrinsically constrained regime of lawful physical causation.

Causal structure is necessary.

But experience is not identical to causal graph form.

It is identical to regime-level intrinsic constraint-bearing operation.

## What This Article Does Not Do

This article does not:

* resolve debates between Humean and non-Humean causation,
* define counterfactual semantics,
* alter admissible-future topology,
* revise the resolution pipeline,
* introduce new metaphysics.

IER remains neutral about the deeper metaphysics of causation.

It assumes only that lawful physical transition exists.

Everything else is structural redescription within that constraint.

## Closing Clarification

Under Informational Experiential Realism:

* Causation is ordinary lawful physical transition.
* Constraint is structural limitation within that law.
* Successor realization, curvature, collapse, and pipeline resolution have distinct physical roles.
* Propagation is the persistence of structural consequence over time.
* Ownership is non-externalizable intrinsic constraint within a globally closed regime.

Nothing additional is added to physics.

Experience does not introduce a new kind of cause.

It is identical to certain regimes of causation - those that are globally closed and intrinsically constrained.

No inflation is permitted.
