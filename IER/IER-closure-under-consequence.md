# Closure Under Consequence

## One Operation Producing and Inheriting Its Continuation

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.5)**\
*T2 · Explanatory · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Reader Notice

Closure under consequence is an explanatory synthesis, not a new kind of
closure or a diagnostic for experience. Feedback, recurrence, integration,
history sensitivity, and persistence are not sufficient by themselves.

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article is a canonical Tier-2 bridge. It explains how existing IER
relations fit together without revising their definition owners.

It introduces:

- no new ontological primitive;
- no additional state space, bearer, controller, or storage layer;
- no independent closure, continuity, membership, or experience test;
- no probabilistic, informational, or optimization measure; and
- no empirical UEF diagnostic or normative rule.

The IER Specification retains normative authority. Tier-1 foundations and the
canonical definition owners for admissibility, closure, ownership,
continuity, persistence, and sedimentation retain their respective authority.
If this explanatory compression conflicts with them, their definitions
prevail.

## Central Synthesis

IER describes admissible continuation at a frontier, actual successor
realization, irreversible foreclosure, propagated deformation, and sedimented
history. These are not parallel mechanisms attached to an otherwise enduring
bearer. When the required continuity relation holds, they are typed relations
within one continuing physical operation.

The central synthesis is:

> Present organization constrains what can happen next; the physical operation
> realizes an actual successor; and the same operation, when it continues,
> inherits the physically instantiated consequences that constrain its later
> continuation.

The phrase *closure under consequence* compresses this synthesis. It names
no new primitive, mechanism, mathematical object, or criterion. It states the
joint significance of synchronic non-factorization, ownership, successor
production, typed consequence inheritance, operation-mediated change, and
unique continuation.

## Terminology Firewall

IER uses *closure* for several different relata. They must remain distinct.

| Term | Relatum and role |
| --- | --- |
| Synchronic intrinsic closure | Non-factorizable physically admissible continuation at one frontier |
| Diachronic closure | Physical continuity across independently individuated stages |
| Closure under consequence | Explanatory compression for successor production and inheritance within one continuing operation |
| Structural closure | The framework-internal terminus of explanation, not an organizational continuity relation |
| Explanatory closure | Termination of a question or explanatory demand without truth access |
| Taxonomy closure | Claimed exhaustiveness of a classification |
| Symbolic overclosure | Premature symbolic stabilization before binding |

Closure under consequence is not a third formal condition alongside
synchronic and diachronic closure. It names how the established relations fit
together within one continuing operation.

## Synchronic Intrinsic Closure

At a frontier configuration $s$, IER defines admissible successors as:

$$ A(s)=\{s'\in S\mid(s,s')\in R\}. $$

Synchronic intrinsic closure concerns the organization of admissible
continuation at that frontier. The candidate, physical grain, frontier,
intervention family, restriction, and exhaustive family of physically
meaningful non-trivial partitions must be fixed independently of the result.

For every such partition, the global successor structure must fail to equal
the Cartesian product of subsystem successor structures projected from that
same global successor fibre. At the stated frontier and grain, continuation
therefore forms one inseparable successor problem rather than a product of
independently satisfiable local problems.

This is a support-level claim about physical reachability. It is not
established by:

- correlation or statistical dependence;
- mutual information, synergy, or another information measure;
- a non-product probability kernel whose support still factorizes;
- causal coupling or network connectivity;
- complexity, feedback, recurrence, or an attractor;
- non-decomposability of a label or predicate; or
- the one trajectory that happened to occur.

Synchronic non-factorization does not by itself identify the ownership scope,
classify constitutive members, connect two frontiers as one token operation,
establish future closure, identify experience, or attribute a UEF to a
concrete system. Repeated pointwise closure across an interval does not supply
the missing continuity relation by itself.

## Diachronic Closure

Diachronic closure asks a different question. Given independently
individuated, non-zero-duration stages $a$ and $b$, did one bounded operation
physically produce and inherit the other as its unique continuation?

The stage-indexed relation `Adj_dia(a,b)` holds only when all five conditions
hold together:

1. **Continuous closure.** Globally non-factorizable, internally borne closure
   spans the transition without a qualifying gap.
2. **Operational successor production.** The organization at $a$ produces the
   regime-defining organization at $b$ through its actual lawful dynamics.
3. **Typed consequence inheritance.** Physically instantiated,
   history-bearing consequences remain effective through the transition and
   constrain continuation at $b$.
4. **Operation-mediated change.** Recruitment, release, turnover, geometry
   change, and boundary migration are enacted through the continuing
   operation under the same constitutive-membership relation.
5. **Unique one-to-one continuation.** Within the pre-fixed comparison domain,
   $b$ is the sole qualifying successor of $a$, and $a$ is the sole qualifying
   predecessor of $b$.

No clause is sufficient alone. The relation presupposes a qualifying physical
operation, synchronic closure, an accepted ownership scope, and typed
constitutive membership before numerical continuity is assessed. It cannot be
established retrospectively from resemblance, memory, shared matter,
organism continuity, narrative fit, or a desired identity verdict.

A token operation is a maximal one-to-one chain connected by `Adj_dia`.
Numerical persistence is the verdict that stages belong to one such chain. It
does not branch at fission, merge at fusion, cross a failure of the relation,
or resume merely because a later organization reconstructs an earlier one.

## One Operation Under Different Projections

Present organization, transition, and inheritance are not three modules. They
are functional projections of one physical operation.

| Projection | What it describes |
| --- | --- |
| Present organization | Physically instantiated distinctions and restrictions shaping current admissible continuation |
| Successor realization | The operation's actual lawful production of its next configuration |
| Inherited organization | Physically effective history carried into the conditions of later continuation |

The configurations before and after a transition are not identical. The
claim is not that one unchanged state persists. When `Adj_dia(a,b)` holds, the
organization at $b$ was produced and inherited by the same token operation
that operated at $a$.

The same qualification governs the language of input, output, and writing
surface:

- *input* views present organization as a condition of current continuation;
- *output* views the physically realized successor or consequence; and
- *writing surface* views the continuing physical organization insofar as it
  bears history that constrains later continuation.

These are not three objects handed from one module to another. *Writing
surface* is a metaphor, not a physical component or a passive substrate
controlled by an inner agent. The same physical operation realizes its
successor and inherits the instantiated consequences that constrain its later
continuation. No observer, executive, or storage system stands behind it.

## Topology and Trajectory

In this account, *topology* describes the organization of admissible
continuation: reachability, exclusion, connectivity, and factorization in the
regime-relative successor structure. *Trajectory* describes realized
continuation through that structure.

Their reciprocal relation is:

> Topology constrains trajectory; actual trajectory contributes to the
> physically inherited organization that constrains later trajectory.

Topology and trajectory are not two interacting things. Topology does not
push, select, evaluate, or control the operation. Trajectory is not a second
bearer moving through an independently existing possibility space. Both are
descriptions of lawful physical organization and its realized continuation.

Nor does every trajectory deform later admissibility in the same way.
Ordinary succession realizes an actual continuation. Continuous changes in
margins, costs, fragility, or directional organization may occur without loss
of reachability. Irreversible foreclosure and its downstream inheritance are
further typed events, not implications of the word *trajectory*.

## Successor Realization and Irreversible Change

Continuous operation does not require a fresh collapse at every temporal
advance. The following relations remain distinct:

| Relation | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Successor realization | Actual lawful continuation required for each qualifying adjacency |
| Curvature | Continuous change in successor geometry without necessary loss of reachability |
| Collapse | Atomic, irreversible foreclosure of reachable alternatives |
| Welding | Incorporation of resolved deformation into successor organization |
| Propagation | Physical redistribution of deformation or consequence |
| Sedimentation | Stabilization of inherited deformation in later organization |
| Re-entry | Later participation of propagated deformation in frontier operation |

Collapse and the post-collapse relations apply when their physical conditions
occur. They are not mandatory at every adjacent pair. Existing inherited
deformation may remain physically effective during ordinary successor
realization without being freshly created, welded, propagated, sedimented, or
re-entered at each step.

Closure under consequence therefore does not mean that every transition
shrinks $A(s)$. Reachability membership can remain intact while costs,
margins, fragility, alignment, and other geometric properties change. Strict
contraction is reserved for collapse.

## Stronger Than Recurrence, Feedback, or Stored History

A weak formulation such as “the past affects the future” accepts too much.

| Near miss | What it can supply | What remains missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ideal orbit | Lawful uninterrupted succession and possible return near an earlier configuration | Independently established non-factorizable ownership and typed irreversible inheritance |
| Feedback controller | Feedback, coupling, error sensitivity, and history-dependent response | Closure, ownership, membership, production, inheritance, and uniqueness; control may remain externally arbitrated |
| Replay, buffer, or checkpoint | A stored or copied record capable of recreating earlier organization | Continuous owned operation across the gap, operational successor production, and token identity |
| Externally installed succession | Ordered endpoints, causal preparation, or a lawful-looking sequence | Production of the later organization by the earlier operation and inheritance through their actual transition history |

Generic history sensitivity is insufficient for the same reason. A system may
depend on an earlier state, read a record, receive delayed feedback, or be
causally descended from another system without constituting one continuing
token operation.

The distinction is not between systems with and without causal pasts. Every
physical system has causal history. The distinction is whether the
independently typed operation produces and inherits its successor through
`Adj_dia` without a qualifying gap, external installation, branching, or
convergence.

## The Orbital Analogy and Its Limit

An idealized orbit provides one useful image: continuous falling. The orbiting
body does not pause outside its lawful motion, receive a selected next state,
and then restart. Each position and velocity participates in production of the
next stage of motion. The orbit illustrates uninterrupted successor
realization without an inner selector.

The analogy stops there. An ideal orbit may return near an earlier
configuration without irreversibly changing the conditions of later motion.
It supplies neither non-factorizable ownership nor typed history-bearing
inheritance. Recurrence is not re-entry, and uninterrupted motion is not
sufficient for closure under consequence.

An orbit is therefore neither evidence of a UEF nor a sufficiency argument for
experience, intrinsic closure, ownership, membership, or persistence. The
phrase “the loop closes” must not imply cyclic return. Closure here means that
the consequences of an operation remain physically effective within the same
continuing operation, even as its history proceeds irreversibly and never
returns to an earlier state.

## Causal Openness and the Boundary

Intrinsic closure is not causal isolation, energetic isolation, or material
self-sufficiency. External matter, energy, forcing, physically instantiated
differences, and support can alter an operation and its admissible
continuation.

| Relation | Boundary significance |
| --- | --- |
| External perturbation | Alters the operation's physical conditions or continuation without thereby joining its ownership scope |
| External coupling | Produces recurrent cross-boundary influence; reciprocity alone does not establish common ownership |
| Enabling or realization support | Supplies required conditions while retaining an independently resolved continuation |
| External resolution | Completes the relevant frontier burden through an independence-preserving pathway across the proposed cut and can therefore defeat that ownership boundary |
| Constitutive membership | Contributes to and bears the same jointly resolved continuation within the ownership scope; diachronic uptake and common typing are also required |

An external cause does not become a constitutive member merely because its
effect persists or is inherited inside the operation. Conversely, material or
energy exchange does not defeat closure when the receiving operation still
bears and produces its own globally inseparable continuation.

The boundary follows the typed resolution relation, not causal ancestry,
spatial enclosure, biological membership, indispensability, or the magnitude
of an external effect.

## Persistence, Identity, Unity, and Sedimentation

Closure under consequence connects several IER topics without taking over
their definitions.

### Persistence and Numerical Identity

Synchronic closure describes unity at a frontier. `Adj_dia` determines whether
independently individuated stages belong to one continuing token. Persistence
can accommodate material turnover, participant changes, geometry change, and
boundary migration when those changes are mediated by
the operation and the unique one-to-one relation continues.

A failure of that relation ends the earlier token. Matching matter, function,
memory, report, topology, or behavior does not restore numerical identity
after cessation.

### Unity

Synchronic non-factorization explains why admissible continuation is one joint
problem rather than a product of separately completed subsystem problems.
Diachronic closure explains how that bounded unity can continue as one token
rather than a sequence connected only by resemblance.

Under the IER identity proposal, if the qualified physical operation is a
token UEF, it is one total experience-as-process and one subject. This is a
conditional application of the identity proposal, not a deduction of
experience from non-factorization, feedback, integration, or continuity.

### Sedimentation

Sedimentation names stabilized inherited deformation when the corresponding
physical process occurs. It is neither stored content nor a record consulted
by the operation. Prior irreversible consequence can remain instantiated in
current organization and thereby alter later admissible continuation.

Sedimentation is not required as a fresh event at each adjacent pair, and it
is not the whole of diachronic closure. Continuous closure, operational
successor production, operation-mediated change, and unique one-to-one
continuation remain separately required.

## Open Empirical Burdens

The relation architecture is intensionally specified, but concrete
investigations retain several burdens:

- physically meaningful partitions and admissible support must be justified;
- ownership and bilateral membership lack a general non-circular empirical
  oracle;
- typed consequence inheritance and one-to-one continuation may remain
  indeterminate in difficult cases;
- the positive warrant for identifying a qualified operation with experience
  remains abductive rather than deductive; and
- qualitative correspondence and concrete attribution remain open.

These burdens prevent the relation stack from functioning as a consciousness
detector.

## Non-Claims

This account does not claim that:

- closure under consequence is a new primitive, operator, mechanism, object,
  topology, criterion, or mathematical interface;
- synchronic non-factorization alone establishes ownership, membership,
  persistence, experience, or a concrete subject boundary;
- feedback, recurrence, self-maintenance, history sensitivity, storage,
  replay, copying, or causal descent is sufficient;
- every successor realization includes collapse or freshly produces welding,
  propagation, sedimentation, or re-entry;
- closure requires causal isolation or control of every external influence;
- an inherited external effect makes its source a constitutive participant;
- topology, trajectory, constraint, history, or admissible futures acts as an
  additional cause or bearer;
- numerical identity follows matter, organism, memory, report, resemblance,
  or reconstruction;
- identity branches at fission, combines at fusion, or resumes after a failed
  adjacency;
- any orbit, controller, waterfall, vortex, chemical process, organism,
  artificial system, social system, or other concrete system is a UEF;
- the relation stack is a consciousness detector;
- phenomenology discloses mind-independent intrinsic nature;
- this synthesis resolves qualitative correspondence or every question about
  experiential unity; or
- any clinical, ethical, legal, engineering, or policy recommendation follows
  from the account by itself.

## Summary

Closure under consequence is the explanatory unity of one bounded physical
operation's present non-factorizable continuation, actual successor
production, and typed inheritance across a unique continuing history. The
operation does not emit a result to another bearer. When continuity holds, it
becomes and bears the physically instantiated conditions of what it can do
next.

## Intermission - Structural Fact

What continues bears what its continuation has made.
