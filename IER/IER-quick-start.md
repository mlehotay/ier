# Quick Start

## Identity, Structure, and Continuity

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.5)**\
*T4 · Orientation · Draft · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article is a compact orientation to Informational Experiential Realism. It introduces no new definitions, criteria, mechanisms, diagnostics, or normative claims. The IER Specification and canonical supporting documents remain authoritative.

## The Claim

IER is a physicalist identity theory of experience.

Its central proposal is:

> A token Unified Experiential Field operation through a continuity interval is identical with total experience-as-process and with the experiential subject.

There is one concrete operation described in three ways:

```text
one token UEF operation
= one total experience-as-process
= one experiential subject
```

The subject is therefore not an additional entity produced by physical dynamics.

Nor is the subject identical merely with:

- the matter supporting the operation;
- an organism or person as ordinarily classified;
- a capacity to sustain experience;
- an instantaneous state; or
- a representation, computation, report, or behavior.

When the qualifying operation exists, its operation does not produce experience.

Its operation is the experience and the subject.

## What Makes a UEF

A Unified Experiential Field is a continuing physical regime under coherent intrinsic constraint.

In a candidate UEF:

- constraint is globally integrated;
- the operation is non-factorizable into independently resolvable subsystem constraints;
- the relevant constraint is generated and borne within the regime;
- it cannot be indefinitely localized, deferred, offloaded, or externally completed;
- the operation persists across a non-zero interval;
- participating processes both contribute to and bear the globally binding constraint; and
- the consequences of prior resolution can remain as organization constraining later continuation.

IER calls the ability to resolve relevant constraint independently **informational slack**.

Where relevant slack remains, the qualifying globally intrinsic regime does not.

Complexity, feedback, recurrence, computation, intelligence, integration, or causal influence are not sufficient by themselves.

UEF boundaries are structural rather than assumed anatomical or spatial boundaries. Bilateral contribution and bearing summarize two necessary aspects of constitutive membership. The full relation also requires joint non-externalizable resolution, diachronic uptake, and common physical typing. All five conditions concern the same operation and restriction; reciprocal influence alone is insufficient. See [Processes](https://github.com/mlehotay/ier/blob/main/IER/IER-processes.md#the-full-membership-relation).

Non-factorization concerns physically admissible successor support, not probability weights or observed correlation. The candidate, variables, grain, interventions, and exhaustive meaningful partition family must be fixed independently; each block is projected from the same outgoing support. See [Intrinsic Closure](https://github.com/mlehotay/ier/blob/main/IER/IER-intrinsic-closure.md).

One independently individuated UEF operation is one subject.

Participation may change without subjecthood becoming graded.

## How the Subject Continues

IER identifies the subject with a temporally extended operation, so persistence depends on continuation of that operation rather than persistence of particular material.

Across successive stages, the same subject continues when:

- one coherent non-factorizable operation remains continuously instantiated;
- the operation produces its own successor through actual dynamics;
- consequences of earlier resolution remain physically inherited by later continuation;
- turnover and boundary change occur through the continuing operation; and
- the later stage is the sole qualifying successor, and the earlier stage its
  sole qualifying predecessor, in a fixed physical comparison domain.

The subject can therefore survive:

- material turnover;
- changing participation;
- changing content;
- internal reorganization; and
- boundary migration.

All five conditions are required. Failure of any condition ends that token.
A genuine UEF-null interval is sufficient for cessation, not necessary.

If the qualifying operation ends and a later UEF begins, the later subject is numerically new even if the substrate, memories, capacities, or apparent continuity remain.

Numerical identity neither branches nor merges. See the full stage relation in
[Continuity](https://github.com/mlehotay/ier/blob/main/IER/IER-continuity.md).

## How the Dynamics Work

IER describes a system in terms of physically admissible continuation.

The present system has a structured set of futures it can physically reach. These are not probabilities, predictions, represented options, or choices presented to an internal decision-maker.

The central diachronic relation is:

> Present topology constrains trajectory, and actual trajectory maintains or changes later topology.

IER distinguishes three important forms of change:

| Process | Structural role |
| --- | --- |
| Successor realization | Lawful actual continuation |
| Curvature | Change in costs, margins, viability, fragility, or alignment while reachability remains |
| Collapse | Irreversible foreclosure in which incompatible continuation becomes unreachable |

After irreversible resolution, its consequences may become part of the organization governing later continuation.

Ordinary successor realization requires no fresh collapse. Previously inherited irreversible consequences can remain physically effective through reversible continuation. The [Futures](https://github.com/mlehotay/ier/blob/main/IER/IER-futures.md#successor-realization) and [Collapse](https://github.com/mlehotay/ier/blob/main/IER/IER-collapse.md#canonical-definition-the-only-unqualified-meaning) articles control these compressed descriptions.

Prior change therefore persists not as a copy of the past but as **sedimented physical deformation of what can happen next**.

This self-inheriting relation is central to IER's account of continuity, memory, agency, and identity.

## What IER Does Not Claim

IER does not provide:

- a general consciousness detector;
- a behavioral, computational, or neural sufficiency test;
- a quale codebook mapping structures to labels such as *red* or *wet*;
- a claim that experience requires cognition, intelligence, language, memory, self-modeling, or reportability; or
- a second mental causal pathway added to physical dynamics.

Qualitative differences are differences in the physical organization of the qualifying operation.

IER does not ask what additional phenomenal property is produced by that organization. On the identity proposal, the physical organization is the qualitative experience.

Concrete UEF attribution may nevertheless remain empirically underdetermined. The theory specifies what must be true of a qualifying operation without claiming that unfamiliar systems can always be externally certified.

## Philosophical Status

IER is an abductive, a posteriori identity proposal.

It can describe its proposed physical regime without using consciousness as a physical input: lawful transitions, admissible continuation, non-factorization, bilateral participation, internally borne resolution, temporal continuity, successor production, and inherited deformation.

That makes the proposed relation schema physically specifiable without using experience as an input. It does not establish a non-circular procedure for identifying subjects in concrete systems: physical variables, boundaries, grain, interventions, and comparison domains must be independently justified.

It does not prove that experience must be identical with that regime.

IER argues instead that one qualifying physical operation and total experience share the relevant identity conditions without requiring an independently existing phenomenal bearer, product, or bridge.
