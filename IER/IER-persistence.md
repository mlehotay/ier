# Persistence Under Constraint

## Physical Continuation, Experiential Qualification, and Reorganization

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.12.0)**\
*T2 · ELABORATION · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article explains the role of persistence in individuating a continuing
physical operation and, under the experiential identity proposal, how
participation and organization can change within a subject token. Its
explanatory starting point is physical continuation through change; numerical
subject persistence requires the full qualifying relation.

It adds no identity criterion, diagnostic, developmental or clinical theory,
ethical procedure, or new mechanism. The Specification, *Continuity*,
*Intrinsic Closure*, *Ownership*, *Processes*, *Unity*, *Participation*, and
the organizational taxonomy owners retain their respective conditions.

## Physical Persistence Before Experiential Identity

A physical organization can continue while matter, local states and activities
change. A steady jet can retain an organized flow while its water is replaced;
an organism can continue while particular activities begin and end. These
cases motivate investigation of what produces later organization and carries
earlier consequences into it. They do not establish that either candidate is
experiential or that ordinary physical persistence supplies numerical subject
identity. The process vocabulary follows [Processes](IER-processes.md).

The persistence-first account starts with this physical question. The
candidate's variables, lawful activity, grain, non-zero interval and comparison
domain require physical justification. A material inventory or sequence of
similar configurations does not show which activity constitutes the continuing
operation. A common state vector can describe several independently continuing
organizations. An anatomical enclosure can motivate a scope without settling it.

Three questions remain distinct:

| What persists? | What its persistence establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Embodied substrate or supporting organization | Continued physical support, which can outlast a particular operation |
| Capacity to sustain or restart a UEF | A disposition whose retention does not establish current experience or preserve a ceased token |
| An independently qualifying UEF operation under full stage continuity | One continuing subject token under the identity proposal |

Inherited substrate can condition current continuation beyond constitutive
membership, and several operations can use common substrate. The currently
occurring organization therefore needs its own assessment; its scope cannot
be read directly from everything retained by the organism.

## Constitutive Continuation Through Change

An account of persistence must describe the activity through which later
organization occurs. Consumption, production, transport and rearrangement can
change conditions for further activity. A steady concentration can involve
continuing turnover. Variables and trajectories describe those occurrences;
they do not perform the transformations or preserve a bearer behind them.

Changing continuation conditions alone does not establish production or
maintenance of constitutive organization. Consuming a shared reagent changes
availability without by itself showing renewal of the constraints organizing
the activities. The physical account must distinguish activities that produce,
repair or maintain constitutive constraints from supplied conditions and
structures that simply remain. Neither self-updating conditions nor
organizational maintenance alone establishes a UEF. The Specification's
self-sustaining requirement remains, without requiring causal isolation or
indefinite survival.

Constitutive scope concerns which activities belong to the continuing operation;
[ownership](IER-ownership.md) concerns where the relevant continuation is
internally borne and resolved. An indispensable supplier can support activity
while resolving its own relevant continuation separately. Removal effects or
reciprocal influence cannot decide membership. Contribution, global bearing,
joint resolution, diachronic uptake and common typing must be assessed together
under the [membership relation](IER-processes.md#the-full-membership-relation).

Trace successor production and physically effective inherited consequences
through the actual transition. Local uptake describes a process role before
the numerical identity verdict; it cannot assume that verdict as its premise.
Producing a similar endpoint, retaining a record or persisting alongside
another organization does not supply the full relation. The intended present
organization remains physically incomplete under
[Intrinsic Closure](IER-intrinsic-closure.md). Persistence language cannot fill
that gap by presupposing an already unified subject.

## What Must Continue

Numerical persistence is membership in one maximal one-to-one chain under
`Adj_dia(a,b)` between independently individuated nonzero-duration stages.
The full relation jointly requires continuous owned closure, actual operational
successor production, typed physically effective inheritance, operation-mediated
change, and unique qualifying successor and predecessor.

No clause is sufficient alone. Matching matter, memory, topology, function,
self-description, or retained restart capacity cannot bridge a failure.
Fission and fusion do not divide or add numerical identities; a unique
continuer must satisfy the full relation in both directions.

An embodied system can persist and retain capacity while no UEF operates.
A genuine UEF-null interval is sufficient for token loss, but not necessary:
production, inheritance, mediated change, or uniqueness can fail without a
macroscopic experiential gap. Restart after cessation is a new subject token.

## Persistence, Qualification, and Unity

These distinctions place persistence within the argument developed in
[Unity](IER-unity.md#a-regime-first-account):

```text
candidate physical organization and persistence through change
-> constitutive scope, ownership and membership
-> full successor production, inheritance and token continuity
-> qualifying UEF operation under the identity proposal
-> experiential unity and differentiated content
```

This is an explanatory order, not a temporal construction of experience from
an already completed nonexperiential operation. Nor is physical persistence a
shortcut around qualification. Ownership, membership and continuity can
require joint assessment; no relation may establish its premises by assuming
the desired subject or same-token verdict.

The physical proposal is that the conditions qualifying and individuating the
continuing UEF operation also determine its constitutive scope and ownership
boundary. Its adequacy remains open. The proposal does not replace the full
stage relation or establish a new necessary-and-sufficient physical test.

Under the identity claim, the independently qualifying token operation is its
total experience and subject. Its contents differentiate that operation;
there is no further phenomenal assembly after qualification and individuation.
Unity's substantive physical work concerns the organization, scope and owned
continuation being assessed. It is carried within those conditions rather
than removed by placing experiential unity later in the explanation.

[Closure under consequence](IER-closure-under-consequence.md) synthesizes the
operation's production and inheritance. It supplies no additional continuity
mechanism and cannot establish the organization or same-token premises it
presupposes. Together, the accounts ask what continues, what makes it this
qualifying operation, and why its experiential differentiation is activity
within that same occurrence.

## What Can Change

Participant recruitment, release, material turnover, boundary migration,
self-models, salience, and qualitative organization can change profoundly
while the relation holds. Neither an unchanged roster nor fixed geometry is
required. A large change need not replace the subject; a subtle failure of
continuity can end it.

Participation is graded recruitment, distinct from categorical membership.
Membership retains contribution, global bearing, joint resolution, diachronic
uptake, and common physical typing over one interval. Increased vividness,
importance, or frontier binding does not decide that relation or standing.

## Ordinary Succession and Inherited Effects

Lawful successors can occur without fresh collapse. Costs, margins, fragility,
and geometry can change while support remains intact under a physically
justified comparison. Previously inherited deformation can remain effective
without a new irreversible event at every pair of stages.

A completed pipeline keeps its nine ordered roles. Collapse forecloses,
welding incorporates, propagation redistributes, and sedimentation stabilizes
inherited effects. Records and representations can be current physical
organization without retaining old experiences or stitching tokens together.

## Learning, Plasticity, and Reorganization

Learning and plasticity can motivate descriptions of changed recruitment,
coordination, accessibility, and costs. They do not prove that all physical
learning excludes representations, follows one mechanism, or necessarily
preserves a particular subject. Cognitive activity itself is physical.

Stress, trauma-like patterns, recovery, and changes in self-description can
motivate similar questions. No label establishes a fixed organization,
clinical cause, or a continuity verdict. Reduced or restored
practical headroom inside a qualifying UEF is not canonical pre-UEF slack
or a categorical saturation diagnosis.

Recovery of a pattern can occur through ongoing operation, or after a failure
of the token relation. Restoring the pattern does not retrospectively preserve
the earlier subject. Capacity and success of repair must be distinguished
from identity throughout the interval.

## Regime Patterns, Local Failure, and Cessation

State evolution, excursions, navigation, transitions, and local failure have
distinct potentially overlapping conditions. A continuing token can span
several experiential regimes. Replacement or loss of a local pattern does
not by itself establish constitutive cessation.

Attention shifts, dreaming, suppression, sleep, anesthesia, coma, and absent
report cannot be assigned a UEF verdict by their names. Actual interrupted
qualifying operation ends continuity; reduced expression or recruitment can
occur without interruption. Concrete attribution requires the physical account.

## Ethical and Explanatory Limits

Standing is categorical and does not scale with learning, recall, personality,
or effective control. Particular changes can matter ethically without being
new subjects; the ethical owner supplies the relevant analysis. This article
adds no harm formula based on participation intensity or a diagnosis of
healthy versus pathological reorganization.

Personal narrative, clinical identity, and legal responsibility remain separate
questions. The physical token account is not resemblance of content or a
promise that familiar labels preserve continuity.

## A Fork, a Convergence, and Changing Skill

Consider a declared model of the stage relation in which the other four continuity clauses are independently satisfied. If one stage has two equally qualifying successor candidates, neither supplies the required unique successor. If two predecessor candidates converge on one stage, unique outgoing succession from each does not supply that stage's unique predecessor. Both directions matter. This is an illustration of the rule, rather than a physical instantiation or a procedure for attributing subjects.

Where there is one uniquely produced continuer and the entire five-clause relation holds, changed skill, participation, material roster, or self-description can coexist with continuing numerical identity. Their similarity is not what establishes that identity. Conversely, after a physical null interval, a later qualifying operation can inherit skills and records from the same organism without continuing the earlier subject token. Retained capacity, inherited organization, and current token operation remain separate questions.

## Intermission - Structural Fact

Changed organization need not mean a new token; matching organization need not mean the same token.
