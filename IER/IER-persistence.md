# Persistence Under Constraint

## Learning and Reorganization Under the Full Token Relation

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.5)**\
*T2 · ELABORATION · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article explains how participation and organization can change within a
continuing subject token. It adds no identity criterion, diagnostic, developmental
or clinical theory, ethical procedure, or new mechanism. The Specification,
*Continuity*, *Processes*, *Participation*, and the organizational taxonomy
owners retain their respective conditions.

## What Must Continue

Numerical persistence is membership in one maximal one-to-one chain under
`Adj_dia(a,b)` between independently individuated nonzero-duration stages.
The full relation jointly requires continuous owned closure, actual operational
successor production, typed physically effective inheritance, operation-mediated
change, and unique qualifying successor and predecessor.

No clause is sufficient alone. Matching matter, memory, topology, function,
self-description, or retained restart capacity cannot bridge a failure.
Fission and fusion do not divide or add numerical identities; a unique
continuer must satisfy the full relation in both directions.

An embodied system can persist and retain capacity while no UEF operates.
A genuine UEF-null interval is sufficient for token loss, but not necessary:
production, inheritance, mediated change, or uniqueness can fail without a
macroscopic experiential gap. Restart after cessation is a new subject token.

## What Can Change

Participant recruitment, release, material turnover, boundary migration,
self-models, salience, and qualitative organization can change profoundly
while the relation holds. Neither an unchanged roster nor fixed geometry is
required. A large change need not replace the subject; a subtle failure of
continuity can end it.

Participation is graded recruitment, distinct from categorical membership.
Membership retains contribution, global bearing, joint resolution, diachronic
uptake, and common physical typing over one interval. Increased vividness,
importance, or frontier binding does not decide that relation or standing.

## Ordinary Succession and Inherited Effects

Lawful successors can occur without fresh collapse. Costs, margins, fragility,
and geometry can change while support remains intact under a physically
justified comparison. Previously inherited deformation can remain effective
without a new irreversible event at every pair of stages.

A completed pipeline keeps its nine ordered roles. Collapse forecloses,
welding incorporates, propagation redistributes, and sedimentation stabilizes
inherited effects. Records and representations can be current physical
organization without retaining old experiences or stitching tokens together.

## Learning, Plasticity, and Reorganization

Learning and plasticity can motivate descriptions of changed recruitment,
coordination, accessibility, and costs. They do not prove that all physical
learning excludes representations, follows one mechanism, or necessarily
preserves a particular subject. Cognitive activity itself is physical.

Stress, trauma-like patterns, recovery, and changes in self-description can
motivate similar questions. No label establishes a fixed organization,
clinical cause, or a continuity verdict. Reduced or restored
practical headroom inside a qualifying UEF is not canonical pre-UEF slack
or a categorical saturation diagnosis.

Recovery of a pattern can occur through ongoing operation, or after a failure
of the token relation. Restoring the pattern does not retrospectively preserve
the earlier subject. Capacity and success of repair must be distinguished
from identity throughout the interval.

## Regime Patterns, Local Failure, and Cessation

State evolution, excursions, navigation, transitions, and local failure have
distinct potentially overlapping conditions. A continuing token can span
several experiential regimes. Replacement or loss of a local pattern does
not by itself establish constitutive cessation.

Attention shifts, dreaming, suppression, sleep, anesthesia, coma, and absent
report cannot be assigned a UEF verdict by their names. Actual interrupted
qualifying operation ends continuity; reduced expression or recruitment can
occur without interruption. Concrete attribution requires the physical account.

## Ethical and Explanatory Limits

Standing is categorical and does not scale with learning, recall, personality,
or effective control. Particular changes can matter ethically without being
new subjects; the ethical owner supplies the relevant analysis. This article
adds no harm formula based on participation intensity or a diagnosis of
healthy versus pathological reorganization.

Personal narrative, clinical identity, and legal responsibility remain separate
questions. The physical token account is not resemblance of content or a
promise that familiar labels preserve continuity.

## Intermission - Structural Fact

Changed organization need not mean a new token; matching organization need not mean the same token.
