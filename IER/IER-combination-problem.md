# The Combination Problem

## Unity Under Closure and Consequence

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.5)**\
*T2 · BRIDGE · Non-Normative · Canon-Constrained*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article explains the accepted IER position. It introduces no primitives,
criteria for experience, diagnostic procedures, or epistemic authority.
*Specification* and the governing canonical owners retain authority.
Its comparisons do not establish novelty or refute every neighboring theory.

## Abstract

IER permits physical composition without phenomenal composition. Experience
and its subject are identical with one globally complete, self-inheriting UEF
operation. Closure under consequence explains how participating physical
differences belong to that continuation without summing subjects or transferring
phenomenal contents. This avoids constitutive combination on its compositional
terms. Synchronic unity and diachronic persistence are intensionally specified;
operationalization, concrete attribution, qualitative correspondence, and the
abductive defense of identity remain open.

## Physical Composition Without Phenomenal Composition

Participating processes can compose a physical operation without its experience
being an aggregate. Their contribution consists in physical differences to one
globally constrained continuation. No phenomenal item must be passed upward,
summed, blended, bonded, or presented to a further observer.

The identity relatum is the globally complete, self-inheriting operation:
the token UEF operation through its continuity interval is the total
experience-as-process and the experiential subject. Substrate, local states,
qualities, and episodes are distinguished from that whole continuing operation.

This argument makes no assumption about component-level experiential status.
Physical parthood is enough to state the compositional distinction.

## The Positive Unity Account

The accepted owners divide the explanation rather than supplying a single
integration score.

* *Intrinsic Closure* specifies synchronic nonfactorization of the
  outgoing continuation fibre across every predeclared physically meaningful
  nontrivial partition, at a fixed candidate, grain, frontier, interval,
  intervention family, and restriction policy. Nonfactorization alone does
  not establish ownership, membership, continuity, or experience.
* *Processes* governs participant membership through contribution,
  global bearing, joint nonexternalizable resolution, diachronic uptake,
  and common typing. Causal contact alone is insufficient.
* *Continuity* independently specifies `Adj_dia(a,b)` between stages
  of nonzero duration. It requires continuous owned closure, operational
  successor production, typed effective inheritance, operation-mediated
  participant change, and unique successor and predecessor within the fixed
  comparison domain. A token is a maximal one-to-one chain.
* *Closure Under Consequence* explains their conjunction. It is an
  explanatory synthesis, not a third criterion or attribution algorithm.

Participating differences matter within the same owned continuation; its global
organization also constrains how they participate. Later organization inherits
physically effective consequences of earlier operation. Inheritance is neither
a copied record nor a requirement for a fresh collapse at every successor.
Cost, margin, participation, and geometry may change while reachability support
persists. An irreversible collapse episode has its own typed contraction;
welding, propagation, and sedimentation carry its consequences into later
admissibility without making all continuation a sequence of collapses.

Unity therefore terminates in a continuing physical operation. “Tied off at
the top” means structural completeness of the owned continuation, not a
particular anatomical level, a central observer, or an exclusion of all nested
physical organization. Ownership conflicts are assessed at the same
organization, resolution, and interval; mere physical overlap is insufficient.

## Separate Combination Problems

The familiar taxonomy distinguishes subject, quality, and structure combination,
with related unity, boundary, grain, and awareness questions.
[Chalmers, “The Combination Problem for Panpsychism”](https://consc.net/papers/combination.pdf)
provides the comparative framing. The dispositions below are IER's own.

### Subject-Summing

Adding subjects does not constitute the subject identified here. The
constitutive summation demand dissolves when phenomenal composition is
rejected. The positive task is to individuate one owned continuing operation
under the accepted relations and defend its identity with experience.

### Phenomenal Bonding

No bond between antecedent phenomenal items assembles the total experience.
IER binding and bonding concern physical participation and consequences within
an operation. They do not name a mechanism for combining experiences.
Their physical explanation remains necessary even though the phenomenal
combination mechanism is unnecessary.

### Palette and Quality

Qualities are organizations within the UEF rather than mixtures of primitive
phenomenal pigments. The palette derivation dissolves. The detailed mapping
from intrinsic-constraint organization to particular qualitative character
remains open; strict identity does not supply that mapping by assertion.

### Grain

There is no requirement to construct a smooth field from phenomenal tiles.
The mosaic demand dissolves in its compositional form. Explaining homogeneity,
differentiation, and apparent granularity within an actual UEF remains a
positive mapping problem.

### Structure

Experiential relations need not preserve a collection of antecedent phenomenal
structures. IER locates structure in organized admissibility and actual
continuation of the UEF. Their detailed correspondence to phenomenal spatial,
temporal, and modal organization remains to be worked out. A graph with a
similar shape does not establish the requisite physical relations.

### Awareness

The operation is not a screen showing assembled contents to another subject.
Phenomenal occurrence is an organization of the operation identical with the
subject. Attention, cognitive access, reportability, and reflection still
require distinct accounts; none is an observer added to make experience occur.

### Unity and Boundary

Synchronic closure specifies the relevant unity intensionally, and diachronic
closure specifies persistence. Membership and owned boundary discipline
restrict which physical contributions belong to the same continuation.
These are partial answers to former unity and boundary burdens, not a general
empirical boundary algorithm. A worked physical instantiation of the full
relation stack and non-gerrymandered attribution remain open.

## What Avoidance Establishes

IER avoids the constitutive combination problem rather than solving it on
compositional terms. Avoidance removes an explanatory demand generated by
phenomenal composition; it does not prove IER's identity thesis.

A critic may still challenge the choice of the whole continuing operation as
the identity relatum, or argue that identity merely redescribes emergence.
IER owes an abductive comparison with constitution and realization, concrete
physical individuation, and qualitative correspondence. The accepted closure
relations make this comparison substantive at the schema level without
settling those research burdens.

## Non-Claims

* Integration does not produce experience as a further result.
* An integration measure or successful model does not diagnose experience.
* Intensional unity does not certify a concrete system as experiential.
* Neither physical similarity nor copied history establishes token identity.
* A branching or merging continuation does not preserve numerical identity
  through multiple successors or predecessors.
* No claim here establishes qualitative correspondence or novelty.

## Intermission - Structural Fact

One continuation can inherit many physical differences without inheriting
a sum of phenomenal contents.
