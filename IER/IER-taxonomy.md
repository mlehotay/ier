# Experiential Taxonomy

## Typed Descriptions of Organization and Change

**Informational Experiential Realism (IER v10.11.5)**\
*T2 · Explanatory · Non-Normative · Non-Criterial*

## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article organizes descriptions of a qualifying Unified Experiential
Field (UEF). It introduces no ontological primitives, attribution tests,
diagnostic thresholds, or ethical permissions. The specification governs the
identity claim; the structural owners named in the dependency metadata govern
their respective relations. Examples illustrate possible descriptions and
do not establish experience, a particular organization, or token continuity.

## Abstract

Experiential taxonomy distinguishes persistent organization, within-regime
state change, returning excursions, reversible navigation, typed resolution,
organizational transition, and failure. These descriptions have different
relata and conditions; they are not six exhaustive mutually exclusive states
of an experiential machine. One interval may instantiate several compatible
roles. Trajectory carries temporal descriptions without becoming a universal
ontological category, and numerical subject continuity remains governed by
the canonical stage relation.

## Why the Earlier Exclusive List Fails

Persistent regime organization can coexist with cognition, navigation,
action, and local failure. Treating these as exactly six mutually exclusive
momentary states mixes an organization with processes, events, and failures
of particular capacities. Temporal composition alone does not resolve that
type mismatch.

The present taxonomy distinguishes descriptive roles. It adds no substances,
forces, controllers, or experiential parts. It makes no proof of an exhaustive
inventory of all possible organizational patterns.

## Organization and Change Matrix

| Description | Relata | Defining condition | Distinction or compatible overlap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Experiential regime | Organization across an interval and conditioned continuation | Persistent organization of borne constraint | Can contain cognition, navigation, action, and local failure |
| State evolution | Successive states relative to a continuing regime | State changes without regime replacement | Can contain excursions and navigation; does not entail collapse |
| Excursion | Departure and return within ongoing organization | Temporary deviation without regime replacement | Returning pattern within state evolution |
| Navigation | Reversible frontier-relevant organization and continuation | Participation, binding, soft bonding, or curvature before any collapse in that episode | Can occur during within-regime evolution; no selector |
| Successor realization | State and actual operative successor | Lawful continuation actually occurs | Does not entail atomic foreclosure |
| Pipeline resolution | Ordered transformations in a conditional episode | Reproduction through sedimentation in the pipeline owner's order | Not required at every successor; can preserve a regime |
| Collapse | Operative reachability before and after a physical event | Atomic irreversible membership contraction | Distinct from cost change, welding, generic breakdown, and cessation |
| Organizational transition | Organization before, during, and after reconfiguration | Specified organization changes, is restored, or is stabilized | Broad family; need not replace regime or contain collapse |
| Regime transition | Different persistent experiential organizations connected by an interval | Regime replacement | Subtype of organizational transition; token may persist |
| Failed transition | Attempted reconfiguration and specified result | Result is not accomplished | Does not entail boundary failure or cessation |
| Boundary failure | Specified demand or capacity and its effective organization | That organization cannot sustain the demand | Can be local while qualifying UEF operation continues |
| UEF cessation / token discontinuity | Qualifying operation or independently individuated stages | Constitutive operation ceases or canonical continuation fails | Not established by local regime loss or later recovery |

The distinctions specify conditions rather than universal duration, intensity,
or phenomenological thresholds. Applying them to a physical case requires
independent description at a declared grain.

## Membership and Geometry

Preserved reachability membership under a justified comparison permits
changing costs, margins, fragility, and accessibility. Unchanged regime
organization does not mean every frontier fibre or geometry is unchanged.
Do not infer collapse from narrower metric appearance or ordinary succession.

Where membership contracts irreversibly, type that event under the collapse
owner; where a complete pipeline is claimed, retain distinct welding,
propagation, and sedimentation roles.

## Cognition and Action

Cognition describes reproduced topology that need not bind to the frontier.
Thinking can recruit participation, binding, soft bonding, and curvature;
it is not exhausted by one operator. Cognitive activity can occur within
regime persistence, excursions, or transition segments.

Owned action is not merely a synonym for collapse. Its irreversible account
includes incorporation through welding and redistribution through propagation,
with sedimentation completing the pipeline description. Action can occur
within a continuing regime and alongside local organizational difficulty.

These role descriptions neither determine UEF presence nor give
phenomenology epistemic authority over mind-independent reality.

## Trajectory as Carrier

A trajectory describes actual physical continuation. A regime transition,
excursion, or state-evolution interval can be a segment of it. Topology
describes organized admissibility; trajectory describes actual continuation.
They do not become separate bearers or a universal class into which regimes,
capacities, and pipeline operators must be reclassified.

## Organizational Continuity and Subject Identity

A token can span several experiential regimes only where independently
individuated stages satisfy `Adj_dia` as owned by `IER-continuity.md`.
Organizational resemblance, unchanged matter, records, recovery, or restart
capacity supply no additional identity test.

Fission and fusion never split or merge numerical identity. Boundary migration
can preserve a token through operation-mediated recruitment and release.
Suppression and dissolution require distinguishing local change from
interrupted qualifying operation; reconstruction does not bridge that
interruption.

## Intermission - Structural Fact

Organizational description and numerical subject continuity answer different
questions about the same physical operation.
