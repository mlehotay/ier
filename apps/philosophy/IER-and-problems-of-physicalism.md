# IER and the Problems of Physicalism

## Status, scope and evidence

Status: bounded assessment; 29 September 2026
Authority: non-canonical; no theory revision or diagnostic

This companion maps the thirty entries in [Problems of Physicalism](problems-of-physicalism.md)
to the implemented IER formulation and accepted research. The original inventory
remains neutral. Numbering refers to its entries, not independent tests that
IER has passed. This is an assessment of commitments and arguments, not empirical
validation or a verdict that physicalism's problems are solved.

The [Specification](../../IER/IER-specification.md) governs normative commitments.
[Intrinsic Closure](../../IER/IER-intrinsic-closure.md),
[Processes](../../IER/IER-processes.md), [Continuity](../../IER/IER-continuity.md)
and [Closure under Consequence](../../IER/IER-closure-under-consequence.md)
own the adopted structural relations. [Reality](../../IER/IER-reality.md),
[Russellian Monism](../../IER/IER-russellian-monism.md),
[Combination Problem](../../IER/IER-combination-problem.md),
[Causation](../../IER/IER-causation.md) and
[Sensory Multiple Realizability](../../IER/IER-sensory-multiple-realizability.md)
supply the bounded explanatory distinctions used below.

The evidence baseline is the [current final research synthesis](strawson-process-subject-final-report.md)
and accepted 44-source antecedents review. The full source audit is private
provenance under `_work/antecedents/`; its corpus is bounded and its count overlaps
with earlier research packages. Phase 9/S5 closed literature comparison and
contribution wording, not independent physical attribution or publication readiness.

Response types are **commitment**, **rejected presupposition**, **specified
relation**, **defeasible argument**, **open burden**, and **separately scoped**.
These labels describe what IER does; they do not show that a critic must agree.
Evidence levels distinguish canonical commitment, articulated physical schema,
source-grounded comparison and concrete demonstration. The accepted S2 case
is only an idealized transport/turnover demonstration, not full-stack evidence.

## Contribution and next evidence

IER contributes the developed framework, its particular formulation,
organization, arguments and implications. Neither articulation of criteria,
formal comparison differences nor an unmatched conjunction establishes first
priority or empirical superiority.

The next substantive evidence should independently justify a concrete physical
candidate, support and partitions, ownership, membership, operational succession,
effective inherited consequences and two-sided uniqueness. Such a case would
strengthen physical application without by itself proving experiential identity.
Quality correspondence and the strongest rival metaphysical arguments require
their own work. This assessment opens no new research batch or release gate.
