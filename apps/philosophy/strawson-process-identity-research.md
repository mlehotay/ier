# Strawson Process-Identity Research

## Status

Status: active research
Authority: non-canonical
Publication: public project stub

This project asks whether Informational Experiential Realism can retain Galen
Strawson's physical process-identity insight without accepting his inference to
panpsychism.

IER accepts that experience and its subject are not entities added to an
underlying physical process. The proposed identity is:

> one continuously operating Unified Experiential Field is the experience and
> the experiential subject.

IER does not infer that fundamental physical constituents are experiential or
that macroexperience is composed from microexperiences or microsubjects. Its
alternative is a locally bounded, system-level physical regime whose unity and
persistence must be explained without experiential microconstituents.

The research is testing whether this combination yields a defensible position:

- Strawson-style physical identity without a separate mental bearer;
- process-subject continuity through changes of content and material
  participation;
- genuine cessation as the end of that subject; and
- rejection of panpsychist constituent inheritance and subject summation.

The project remains in research. It does not modify canonical IER or claim that
the argument against panpsychism is complete.
