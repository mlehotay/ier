## Zero-result and high-noise patterns

The literal strings `"numerically identical" subject consciousness process`
and `"experiential subject" process physicalism` produced no additional
verified Level A source beyond the Strawson cluster. Variants using *owner* and
*experiencer* mostly returned grammar, theology, legal identity, clinical
consciousness, or generic hard-problem discussions. Variants using *field* and
*operation* disproportionately returned physical-field theories and
computational descriptions that did not address subject ontology.

## Citation-chain stopping record

Backward and forward chains were followed from Strawson 2003 through Dainton
2012/2016, Taylor 2020, later Strawson collections, O'Conaill 2020 as a
critical lead, and recent subject-counting/overlap literature. Two successive
rounds returned:

- restatements or objections to Strawson's subject–experience identity;
- phenomenal-self or capacity theories;
- panpsychist combination problems;
- unity and counting debates; and
- generic process or state-identity claims.

No new source in those rounds explicitly combined (1) the concrete
experiential subject, (2) a particular physical process, (3) numerical
identity, and (4) diachronic continuation through changing experience. The
main search was therefore stopped under the plan's repetition and
nearest-precedent rules. The inaccessible Owen 2024 text remains an access
qualification, but the claim needed for the main classification is independently
settled by Strawson's full primary text.
