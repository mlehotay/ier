# Philosophy of Mind

Reading guides and research on IER's place in philosophy of mind, with a focus
on experiential unity, subject identity, and continuity through change.

## Status

Status: support\
Authority: non-canonical\

These materials explain and assess IER. The
[Specification](../../IER/IER-specification.md) governs the theory's criteria.

## Research context

The research connects Strawson's physical process-subject identity and
Papineau's system-level identity with antecedents in cybernetics, organizational
biology, and accounts of persistence. The reports distinguish those precedents
from IER's particular formulation and arguments; they do not establish
historical first priority.

The final report records the accepted synthesis and remaining research
obligations. Original source findings and dated comparisons remain historical
evidence. Public references are included in the reports; detailed source
ledgers, notes, and decision records remain in the private development workspace.
