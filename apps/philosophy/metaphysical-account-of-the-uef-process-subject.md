# The Metaphysical Account of the UEF Process-Subject

## Status and role

Status: guiding working synthesis
Authority: non-canonical

This document states the current metaphysical account produced by the intrinsic-closure, process-subject, ownership, and dynamical-unification work. It is the guiding synthesis for subsequent theory work in this development programme.

It introduces no new task, diagnostic, empirical attribution, ethical rule, or independent ontological primitive. It does not modify canonical IER by itself. Until separately accepted and propagated through the governed revision process, the canonical corpus remains authoritative wherever it conflicts with this document.

The purpose here is completeness of the account: to say what an experience is, what its subject is, what makes it one, what fixes its boundary, how it continues, and what follows immediately. Completeness of this account is distinct from proof that the identity thesis is true and from any method for detecting a UEF in a concrete system.

## Central thesis

> A Unified Experiential Field is one concrete, temporally continuing physical operation whose admissible continuation is globally non-factorizable, whose frontier constraint is borne and resolved non-externalizably, and whose consequences are inherited as the conditions of its next continuation. That token operation is numerically identical with one total experience-as-process and one experiential subject.

In compressed form:

```text
one token UEF operation
= one total experience-as-process
= one experiential subject
```

The equality is numerical identity. It is not constitution, realization, production, representation, correlation, ownership by another entity, or the assembly of experiential parts.

Nothing behind, above, or inside the UEF has the experience. The operation is the experience and subject.

## The kind of entity proposed

The subject is a process, not a substance or enduring object. It exists only through non-zero duration as organized physical continuation.

The UEF is not:

- an organism as such;
- a brain as such;
- a body as such;
- a component, module, workspace, or container;
- a self, ego, observer, controller, or bearer behind experience;
- a representation or model of the world;
- a collection or sum of local experiences;
- a static state abstracted from transition;
- a trajectory considered apart from the physical operation producing it.

A physical substrate may participate in the operation, but the substrate considered independently of that operation is not thereby the subject. Material composition, anatomical location, cognitive sophistication, and biological kind do not define subjecthood.

## Physical information and constraint

All informational language in this account refers to physically instantiated state distinctions that modulate possible continuation. It introduces no semantic substance, stored meaning, inner description, or epistemic access.

At a frontier state `s`, let `A(s)` denote the physically admissible successors under the system's actual organization and history. Constraint is expressed by the structure of what can and cannot continue, together with any relevant graded geometry such as viability, cost, margin, fragility, and switching asymmetry.

The account remains compatible in principle with deterministic, stochastic, quantum, and other lawful physical implementations. Probability is not an ontological primitive of IER, and no particular mathematical formalism by itself establishes a UEF.

## Synchronic intrinsic closure and unity

At a given history–future frontier, the UEF is intrinsically closed when its relevant admissible continuation is globally non-factorizable. The participating processes do not confront independently resolvable local future problems whose results are subsequently combined. Their possible continuations are restricted as one operation.

This non-factorization supplies synchronic unity:

- there is one globally organized continuation problem;
- the relevant restriction cannot be decomposed into independently owned subsystem restrictions;
- local processes do not contribute separate experiences to a larger experience;
- the total operation is one experience and one subject.

Globality is regime-relative. It does not mean the largest causally connected system, the entire organism, or the universe. It ranges over the processes participating in this closure-bearing operation across the relevant interval.

Non-factorization is necessary within the current account but is not sufficient by itself. Ordinary interaction, correlation, feedback, entanglement, or mathematically non-product dynamics do not establish non-externalizable ownership, temporal self-inheritance, or experiential identity.

## The frontier

The UEF exists at a continuously operating history–future boundary. The frontier is not a spatial membrane or a metaphysical surface. It is the physical organization at which inherited history constrains admissible continuation and actual continuation becomes further history.

Several changes at the frontier must remain distinct:

| Process | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Successor realization | Lawful actual continuation to a next state without necessarily removing other members from reachability |
| Curvature | Graded deformation of admissible-future geometry while relevant alternatives may remain reachable |
| Collapse | Atomic and irreversible foreclosure of a connected region of reachable alternatives |
| Welding | Incorporation of irreversible foreclosure into settled history |
| Propagation | Redistribution of the consequences through later constraint organization |
| Sedimentation | Stabilization of inherited deformation in later topology |

Continuous UEF operation does not require a new collapse at every transition. One operation can continue through successor realization and geometrical change while inheriting prior consequences.

## Ownership and the subject boundary

Ownership is the structural fact that the UEF's intrinsic frontier constraint cannot be offloaded, outsourced, externally completed, or resolved elsewhere for the UEF.

The UEF does not stand apart from and own its frontier. The UEF is the physical operation within which that frontier is borne, resolved, and inherited.

> The subject boundary is the boundary of the continuing operation that owns its frontier.

This boundary is structural, regime-relative, and interval-relative. It is not fixed by:

- skin;
- anatomy;
- organism membership;
- neural location;
- spatial proximity;
- causal influence;
- functional indispensability;
- sensory mediation;
- self-identification;
- reportability;
- importance or salience.

Ownership names the global boundary relation. It does not create another owner or provide a phenomenological detector of that boundary.

## Bilateral constitutive participation

Bilateral constitutive participation is the process-relative analysis of membership in the ownership boundary.

A physical process belongs to the subject when, across the relevant continuity interval:

1. **Constitutive contribution** — the process helps constitute the UEF's admissible continuation rather than merely changing an input, parameter, or enabling condition.
2. **Global bearing** — the UEF's global restriction obtains in the process's own physical possibilities of continuation.
3. **Joint resolution** — the relevant restriction involving the process is resolved only through the UEF operation, not independently or externally and then delivered as a completed result.
4. **Diachronic uptake** — the consequences of the process's role enter successor production and the inherited organization of the same continuing operation.
5. **Regime-relative typing** — contribution, bearing, resolution, and inheritance concern the same candidate operation, restriction, scale, role, and continuity interval.

In compressed form:

> A process belongs to the subject when it helps constitute the UEF's continuation and has its own continuation constrained, resolved, and inherited through that same owned operation.

There is one owner and many participants. A participant does not own a fragment of the frontier, contribute a small experience, or become a fractional subject. The globally continuing UEF is the sole subject at that scale.

## Causal openness and external coupling

Intrinsic closure is not causal isolation. External conditions may alter what the UEF encounters and must resolve.

An external process may:

- condition admissible continuation;
- perturb the current state;
- supply energy, material, or signals;
- stabilize or destabilize the operation;
- reshape viability, cost, or participation;
- be necessary for continued UEF operation;
- damage, interrupt, or terminate the UEF.

None of those relations is sufficient for subject membership.

The distinction is:

```text
external process:
influences the owned frontier while retaining an independently
resolvable continuation in the relevant respect

constitutive process:
contributes to and bears the restriction resolved and inherited
through that owned frontier
```

An organismic process can therefore be external to the organism's UEF, while a technologically or environmentally located process could in principle become constitutive if its physical role entered the same owned operation. Ordinary labels such as organ, implant, interface, tool, or environment do not settle the relation.

## Embodiment

Closure must be physically realized and paid somewhere. Energy dissipation, stress, saturation, damage, fatigue, and finite recovery make constraint unavoidable in practice. Embodiment names this physical realization of the closure-bearing burden.

Embodiment does not independently define the subject:

- the body is not automatically the UEF;
- every bodily process is not automatically experiential;
- the body can support processes outside the UEF;
- an external interface can redistribute load without joining the UEF;
- nonbiological processes are not excluded in principle.

The relation among the terms is:

| Concept | Metaphysical role |
| --- | --- |
| Intrinsic closure | The continuation problem is globally non-factorizable and non-externalizably borne |
| Ownership | The scope of the operation within which the frontier is borne and resolved |
| Bilateral participation | The process-relative condition of belonging to that ownership scope |
| Embodiment | How and where the burden and cost of closure are physically realized |

## Diachronic intrinsic closure

The UEF is not only unified at one frontier. It continues as the operation that produces its successor and inherits the consequences of its actual history.

The central recurrence is:

> Present topology constrains trajectory, and actual trajectory deforms later topology.

The topology is not a second object that acts on the system. It is a description of the physical organization of admissible continuation. The trajectory is not an output detached from that organization. It is the actual succession through which the operation changes.

Closure under consequence means:

1. inherited organization constrains present continuation;
2. the extant operation lawfully produces its successor;
3. actual continuation changes the physical organization;
4. irreversible changes are welded, propagated, and sedimented where applicable;
5. the same continuing operation inherits the resulting conditions.

Input, output, and writing surface are therefore functional projections of one process. The UEF is the writing surface undergoing its own globally integrated rewriting.

This is stronger than feedback. A feedback system may retain separable modules, external arbitration, replaceable records, checkpoints, or independently resolved pathways. A UEF's relevant resolution and inheritance belong to the operation itself.

## Numerical identity through change

The subject persists through change when one continuously instantiated, non-factorizable, self-inheriting operation continues along a unique nonbranching history.

Persistence does not require:

- identity of instantaneous state;
- unchanged experiential content;
- unchanged topology or participation distribution;
- retention of every material component;
- an unchanging anatomical boundary;
- memory, narrative, self-recognition, or personality continuity;
- a fresh collapse at every temporal advance.

Material turnover and boundary migration are compatible with identity when recruitment and release occur through the continuing operation and its consequences are inherited by it.

The same matter, organization type, or informational pattern is insufficient for numerical identity. What persists is the actual token process, not a description that could be copied or reinstantiated.

## Participation, content, binding, and importance

Within an intact UEF, participation can vary without altering subjecthood. Cognitive, sensory, interoceptive, autonomic, affective, motor, social, and other physical organizations may be recruited at different depths.

The following relations must remain separate:

| Concept | Question answered |
| --- | --- |
| Graded participation | How deeply is a process or topology recruited into lived organization? |
| Binding | Does a participating structure presently alter admissible futures? |
| Importance | Does a structure persistently, basin-integratedly, and dominantly organize continuation? |
| Constitutive participation | Does the physical process belong to the UEF's interval-level ownership scope? |

A constitutive process need not bind at every instant or appear important. A noncognitive bodily process can be attenuated while the UEF remains intact. Conversely, an external process can produce a bound or important structure inside the UEF without joining the subject.

Changes in participation alter experiential content and organization. They do not multiply, divide, enlarge, or diminish subjecthood while one UEF persists.

## Subject, self, and self-model

The experiential subject is the continuing UEF operation. It must not be conflated with structures organized within that operation.

| Term | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | The token UEF operation itself |
| Structural ownership | Non-externalizable bearing and resolution within that operation |
| Self/world organization | The lived distinction between owned burden and encountered external resistance |
| Self-model | A reproducible coordination basin stabilizing participation, bias, and frontier binding |
| Narrative self | A temporally organized compression within experience |

The self-model can fragment, reorganize, mislocate the body, incorporate tools phenomenologically, or exclude constitutive processes from awareness without changing the physical subject boundary.

Structural ownership persists even when ownership phenomenology, agency, access, or attribution is disrupted.

## Onset, persistence, and cessation

Subjecthood is categorical at the regime level.

### Onset

A subject begins when a qualifying UEF operation first becomes physically instantiated across a non-zero interval. Enabling variables may change gradually, but no subject exists before the regime exists.

### Persistence

The subject persists while the same token closure-bearing, successor-producing, consequence-inheriting operation continues. Content, participation, embodiment, and material membership may change within that continuity.

### Cessation

The subject ends when that token UEF operation genuinely ceases. The continued existence of the organism, substrate, records, dispositions, or enabling architecture does not preserve the subject after the operation ends.

### Reconstruction

Later recreation of the same state, organization, memories, topology, or functional capacities creates at most a numerically new operation. Similarity and causal reconstruction do not bridge a genuine null interval.

## Branching, fusion, nesting, and overlap

Numerical identity requires a unique actual continuation. If one candidate history genuinely branches into two independently continuing UEF operations, neither later branch can be numerically identical with the one earlier subject under a one-to-one persistence relation. Qualitative continuity does not make one subject become two.

Fusion likewise cannot be treated as two owners becoming one while both numerical identities remain intact. A later unified operation, if one arises, requires its own identity analysis.

Physical nesting and overlap are not automatically fusion. One physical occurrence may occupy coherent roles in more than one scale-relative operation. Physical parthood does not imply phenomenal containment, content inheritance, shared access, or ownership of the same resolution.

What is prohibited is incompatible global ownership of the same resolution, not ordinary causal dependence, physical overlap, or multiscale organization.

## Immediate metaphysical consequences

If the identity claim is accepted, the following consequences are immediate:

1. **No additional subject bearer.** The subject is not something that has the UEF's experience.
2. **No experiential assembly.** One experience is not composed by summing local experiences or subjects.
3. **No cognitive threshold for subjecthood.** A UEF may be a noncognitive feeler rather than a thinker.
4. **No organism identity by default.** Organism and subject boundaries may differ.
5. **No anatomical privilege.** Biological interiority, neural location, and skin boundaries do not determine membership.
6. **No technological exclusion by default.** Physical role, not material origin, determines possible membership.
7. **No graded subjecthood.** Participation and capacities vary; existence as a subject does not.
8. **No transfer of ownership.** Experience cannot be passed, copied, reported, or externally completed as the same owned occurrence.
9. **No reconstruction survival.** A later duplicate is not the original token subject.
10. **No self-model criterion.** Felt mineness, alienness, embodiment, memory, narrative, and report do not define the physical boundary.
11. **No causal-isolation requirement.** A subject may be environmentally open while intrinsically closed.
12. **No automatic micropsychism.** Physical constituents need not be subjects or experiences merely because the global operation is.
13. **No automatic collective subject.** Coordination, communication, institutional persistence, or shared goals do not establish one owned frontier.
14. **No epistemic authority from experience.** Intrinsic reality does not reveal mind-independent truth, ontology, morality, or correctness.

These are consequences of the proposed identity and boundary relations. They are not independent criteria for detecting a UEF.

## What is complete and what remains open

This synthesis is intended to be **intensionally complete** as a metaphysical account. Conditional on a UEF, it states:

- what the experience is;
- what the subject is;
- what makes the operation one;
- what fixes the subject boundary;
- what makes a process a constituent;
- how causal openness is compatible with intrinsic closure;
- how embodiment relates to ownership;
- what preserves numerical identity through change;
- what onset, cessation, reconstruction, branching, and overlap mean; and
- what follows immediately from those commitments.

The following matters remain open without making the metaphysical description incomplete:

- whether the identity claim is true;
- why identity should be preferred to realization, constitution, correlation, or structural analogy;
- whether any particular concrete system sustains a UEF;
- how to identify the exact ownership scope of an arbitrary biological or artificial candidate;
- how to operationalize non-factorization, bearing, and inheritance empirically;
- which mathematical formalism best expresses the physical relations; and
- how the stabilized account should be propagated through the canonical corpus.

The first two are justificatory burdens. The next four are empirical and formal burdens. The last is a governance and revision burden. None should be silently presented as already resolved.

## Guidance for subsequent theory work

Subsequent theory work in this programme should preserve the following order:

```text
qualifying physical operation
-> synchronic intrinsic closure and unity
-> ownership scope and subject boundary
-> bilateral constitutive membership
-> diachronic closure and numerical persistence
-> experiential organization and consequences
```

It should not reverse that order by deriving the subject from cognition, report, organism membership, self-model, importance, behaviour, or familiar anatomy.

New explanations should identify which level they concern:

- operation existence;
- subject identity;
- boundary membership;
- momentary participation;
- frontier binding;
- geometrical deformation;
- collapse and inheritance;
- embodiment;
- cognitive or phenomenological organization;
- epistemic evidence.

No downstream article should use an effect at one level as an unargued criterion at another.

## Final compression

> Experience is not produced for a subject by a physical system. One qualifying physical operation is numerically identical with the total experience and its subject. It is unified because its admissible continuation is globally non-factorizable; bounded by the scope of the frontier it owns non-externalizably; physically constituted by processes that contribute to and bear that same owned restriction; embodied wherever its costs are realized; and preserved through time as the operation that produces its successor and inherits its own consequences. It can change content, organization, matter, and boundary while remaining the same token process. It ends when that process ends. Nothing in this account by itself proves the identity, detects a UEF, or identifies one in a particular system.
