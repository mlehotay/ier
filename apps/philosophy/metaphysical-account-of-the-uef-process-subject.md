# The Metaphysical Account of the UEF Process-Subject

## Status and role

Status: current philosophical exposition; synchronized 12 November 2026
Authority: non-canonical; canon-constrained

This document explains the v10.12.0 account of experience, subject, unity,
ownership, membership and continuity, using the persistence-first explanatory
order in the maintained core argument, `traceability/claims-summary.md`
(development repository).
It develops the author's chosen philosophical approach: experiential unity
follows from identity with a qualifying continuing operation, rather than
requiring a further assembly relation. The physical proposal is that the
conditions qualifying and individuating the continuing operation also determine
its constitutive scope and ownership boundary. The adequacy of those physical
conditions remains open.
Phases 1–9/S1–S5 are complete at their recorded bounds; that historical status
does not establish completion of the revised physical account. This exposition
introduces no criterion, diagnostic, empirical attribution, ethical rule or
independent ontological primitive.

## Central thesis

> A Unified Experiential Field is a concrete, temporally continuing physical operation whose intrinsic constraint is generated and maintained, whose frontier constraint is borne and resolved non-externalizably, and whose consequences are inherited through its qualifying continuation. That token operation is numerically identical with one total experience-as-process and one experiential subject. Experiential unity is the oneness of this qualifying operation, whose differentiated activities constitute its content.

In compressed form:

```text
one token UEF operation
= one total experience-as-process
= one experiential subject
```

The equality is numerical identity. It is not constitution, realization, production, representation, correlation, ownership by another entity, or the assembly of experiential parts.

Nothing behind, above, or inside the UEF has the experience. The operation is the experience and subject.

## Identity and unity: the same philosophical strategy

The identity proposal rejects a picture in which a physical operation first
occurs and then produces an additional experiential occurrence for a separate
bearer. Physical operation, total experience and subject name the same token.
On this proposal, no further production bridge connects the physical occurrence
to experience. This is IER's approach to the hard problem, not a proof that the
identity is true or that every objection to it has been answered.

The approach to unity uses the same strategy. It rejects a starting picture
in which independently existing experiences must acquire a further bond to
become one total experience. Given identity with a qualifying operation,
experiential differences are differentiations within that operation. There is
no subsequent phenomenal assembly step. Attention, binding and changing
participation organize the content; they do not supply its oneness.

There is no further assembly work after the operation has been qualified and
individuated, but unity still has substantive explanatory work. The physical
account must explain what makes the candidate one continuing operation, how
its differentiated activities belong together, and where its constitutive
scope lies. The persistence-first order places that work within qualification
and individuation. Beginning with a candidate continuing organization does
not establish its oneness merely by naming it.

| Explanatory question | Identity-based account | Remaining obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How does physical activity produce experience for a subject? | The qualifying operation is the experience and subject. | Specify the physical relatum and defend the identity proposal. |
| How do experiential differences form one experience? | They are differences within that same qualifying operation. | Physically individuate the operation and its constitutive scope. |

The philosophical consequence and the physical proposal must remain distinct.
Under the identity proposal, one qualifying token operation is one total
experience. The physical proposal is that the conditions qualifying and
individuating the continuing UEF operation also determine its constitutive
scope and ownership boundary. Under the identity claim, experiential unity
follows without an additional unity condition. The adequacy of the physical
conditions remains open. In the development repository, the current conceptual
contract (`_work/unity/propagation/conceptual-contract.md`) states this research
direction; the continuation-domain hypothesis
(`_work/unity/discovery/unity-as-continuation-domain.md`) records its exploratory
background. These development references are not included in the public export.

The constitutive scope concerns processes belonging to that operation over the
relevant interval. The ownership boundary concerns where its continuation is
internally borne and resolved. These are related descriptions with distinct
physical obligations. They do not automatically include the entire sedimented
substrate: inherited organization can condition continuation outside current
membership, and a shared substrate can support several operations. Sedimented
capacity and the currently occurring operation must remain distinguishable.

Calling a collection “one operation” cannot discharge this obligation. A common
state vector or update function can describe independent systems. The account
must identify the continuing organization and its scope without assuming the
subject whose unity it is meant to explain. Any additional inseparability
condition must earn its role through physical and explanatory justification.

## Begin with physical persistence

First describe a candidate organization that continues through changing states,
matter and participating activities. Ask what produces later organization,
which constraints are generated or maintained, and how consequences remain
physically effective. An organism or an anatomically motivated region can
provide a starting candidate without already defining the experiential scope.

Ordinary persistence does not establish experience or numerical subject
continuity. Nor does changing conditions of later activity establish maintenance
of the organization constituting that activity. Constitutive membership,
external support, ownership and the full stage relation retain their separate
obligations. This order lets those physical relations be investigated before
invoking experiential unity as their consequence.

## Unity's physical work within persistence and qualification

The earlier regime-first investigation developed physical questions that remain
essential to this account. Their place in the argument changes: they concern
the organization being qualified and individuated, rather than an additional
unity relation applied after that work is complete.

First, describe the activity through which the organization continues.
Concentrations, contacts, positions and stresses condition consumption,
production, transport and rearrangement; these activities change conditions
for later activity. Variables describe those conditions, while processes
perform the transformations. A steady concentration can involve continuing
turnover. Ordinary physical interactions account for these changes, without
a separate updater or informational force.

Then distinguish changing continuation conditions from producing or maintaining
the organization through which activity occurs. Consuming a shared reagent
changes another reaction's possibilities, but does not by itself demonstrate
renewal of the reaction network's constitutive constraints. Trace which
activities produce, repair or maintain those constraints, which conditions
are supplied externally, and which structures simply persist. Self-updating
conditions alone establish neither the Specification's self-sustenance nor
numerical persistence; organizational maintenance alone does not establish
experience.

The account must also explain the proposed scope of joint activity. Mediated,
directional and uneven dependencies can matter without direct all-to-all
influence. A common material, reciprocal causal arrows or a combined equation
does not establish that the activities constitute one operation. Shared-reagent
reactions, responsive baths, external suppliers, independent remainders and
two interacting organizations supply comparisons through which to investigate
the distinction. A jointly organized subcluster cannot establish membership
of an added remainder. These questions require physical justification of grain,
interval and scope; they do not reinstate a standing requirement of
inseparability across every meaningful partition.

Ownership and membership make this scope question more precise. An indispensable
supplier can affect the candidate and receive its effects while resolving its
relevant continuation separately. Removal alone cannot distinguish support from
constitution. Replaying one trajectory is also insufficient: a replacement that
must reproduce removed joint dynamics may have relocated the resolver. The
five membership clauses below require assessment together under common physical
typing. Attention and prominence cannot substitute for those relations.

Finally, trace how activity produces successor organization and how earlier
consequences remain physically effective within it. Local uptake, retained
substrate, similar endpoints and copied records do not establish all five
conditions of numerical continuity. Closure under consequence synthesizes the
assessed organization, production and inheritance; it cannot establish its
own premises. Membership cannot assume the very same-token verdict that its
physical uptake relation helps constrain.

These are explanatory obligations within the physical proposal. The relation
between them can require joint assessment and revision, so their presentation
as a sequence does not make each independently settled. Their adequacy remains
open. Under identity, experiential unity articulates the oneness that this
physical account must justify; it adds no further bond among antecedent
experiences.

## The kind of entity proposed

The subject is a process, not a substance or enduring object. It exists only through non-zero duration as organized physical continuation.

The UEF is not:

- an organism as such;
- a brain as such;
- a body as such;
- a component, module, workspace, or container;
- a self, ego, observer, controller, or bearer behind experience;
- a representation or model of the world;
- a collection or sum of local experiences;
- a static state abstracted from transition;
- a trajectory considered apart from the physical operation producing it.

A physical substrate may participate in the operation, but the substrate considered independently of that operation is not thereby the subject. Material composition, anatomical location, cognitive sophistication, and biological kind do not define subjecthood.

## Physical information and constraint

All informational language in this account refers to physically instantiated state distinctions that modulate possible continuation. It introduces no semantic substance, stored meaning, inner description, or epistemic access.

At a frontier state `s`, let `A(s)` denote the physically admissible successors under the system's actual organization and history. Constraint is expressed by the structure of what can and cannot continue, together with any relevant graded geometry such as viability, cost, margin, fragility, and switching asymmetry.

The account remains compatible in principle with deterministic, stochastic, quantum, and other lawful physical implementations. Probability is not an ontological primitive of IER, and no particular mathematical formalism by itself establishes a UEF.

## Synchronic intrinsic closure and unity

The [intrinsic-closure owner](../../IER/IER-intrinsic-closure.md) now describes
present unity as one self-updating organization whose constituent activities
jointly transform the conditions of their own continuation. Synchronic closure
names the intended joint organization under intrinsic constraint. This is an
incomplete physical formulation: its constitutive boundary, joint resolution,
and relation to organizational maintenance remain open.

The v10.12.0 correction withdrew successor-support non-factorization as a
necessary unity condition. A singleton `A(s)` factorizes regardless of the
productive dependencies that yield its successor. Product support does not
establish disunity, and non-product support does not establish unity. The
support calculations retain their mathematical meaning without individuating
a subject. Whether resistance to every meaningful partition is justified as
an additional physical requirement remains unresolved.

Candidate scope, variables, grain, interval and admissible comparisons must
receive physical justification independently of the desired verdict. Coupling,
feedback, correlation and organizational maintenance alone do not establish a
UEF. Ownership, membership, self-sustenance and full continuity retain their
requirements; no proposed local relation silently discharges the others.

Under the identity proposal, differentiated experience is activity within the
qualifying operation. Unity follows at that level. The unresolved physical
question is whether the proposed qualification and individuation conditions
adequately establish that operation, its constitutive scope and ownership
boundary. An additional unity condition is not assumed. Globality is
relative to its constitutive scope, not the largest connected system, the
entire organism or the universe.

This retains a distinction between the explanandum and its proposed account.
Phenomenal unity names the question of experiences occurring together as a
whole; experiential unity names the oneness of differentiated experience;
regime unity articulates that oneness as the qualifying physical operation.
Access, binding, representation and homogeneous content answer different
questions. Access unity or disunity alone does not decide experiential oneness.

## The frontier

The UEF exists at a continuously operating history–future boundary. The frontier is not a spatial membrane or a metaphysical surface. It is the physical organization at which inherited history constrains admissible continuation and actual continuation becomes further history.

Several changes at the frontier must remain distinct:

| Process | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Successor realization | Lawful actual continuation to a next state without necessarily removing other members from reachability |
| Curvature | Graded deformation of admissible-future geometry while relevant alternatives may remain reachable |
| Collapse | Atomic and irreversible foreclosure of a connected region of reachable alternatives |
| Welding | Incorporation of irreversible foreclosure into settled history |
| Propagation | Redistribution of the consequences through later constraint organization |
| Sedimentation | Stabilization of inherited deformation in later topology |

Continuous UEF operation does not require a new collapse at every transition. One operation can continue through successor realization and geometrical change while inheriting prior consequences.

## Ownership and the subject boundary

Ownership is the structural fact that the UEF's intrinsic frontier constraint cannot be offloaded, outsourced, externally completed, or resolved elsewhere for the UEF.

The UEF does not stand apart from and own its frontier. The UEF is the physical operation within which that frontier is borne, resolved, and inherited.

> The subject boundary is the boundary of the continuing operation that owns its frontier.

This boundary is structural, regime-relative, and interval-relative. It is not fixed by:

- skin;
- anatomy;
- organism membership;
- neural location;
- spatial proximity;
- causal influence;
- functional indispensability;
- sensory mediation;
- self-identification;
- reportability;
- importance or salience.

Ownership names the global boundary relation. It does not create another
owner or provide a phenomenological detector. Within the candidate continuing
organization, independently establish where the relevant burden is borne and resolved;
the desired subject attribution cannot select the ownership scope. Maximality
concerns compatible constitutive participation in that scope and interval,
not the widest causal ancestry or largest connected system.

## Bilateral constitutive participation

Bilateral constitutive participation is the process-relative analysis of membership in the ownership boundary.

A physical process belongs to the subject when, across the relevant continuity interval:

1. **Constitutive contribution** — the process helps constitute the UEF's admissible continuation rather than merely changing an input, parameter, or enabling condition.
2. **Global bearing** — the UEF's global restriction obtains in the process's own physical possibilities of continuation.
3. **Joint resolution** — the relevant restriction involving the process is resolved only through the UEF operation, not independently or externally and then delivered as a completed result.
4. **Diachronic uptake** — the consequences of the process's role enter successor production and the inherited organization of the same continuing operation.
5. **Common typing** — contribution, bearing, resolution, and uptake concern the same operation, restriction, variables, grain, scale, regime, intervention family, and interval.

In compressed form:

> A process belongs to the subject when it helps constitute the UEF's continuation and has its own continuation constrained, resolved, and inherited through that same owned operation.

All five clauses are jointly required by [Processes](../../IER/IER-processes.md).
Reciprocal causal arrows, necessity or momentary coupling alone are insufficient.
There is one owner for this operation and many participants. A participant does
not own a fragment of its frontier, contribute a small experience, or become a
fractional subject. Other operations require independent individuation.

## Causal openness and external coupling

Intrinsic closure is not causal isolation. External conditions may alter what the UEF encounters and must resolve.

An external process may:

- condition admissible continuation;
- perturb the current state;
- supply energy, material, or signals;
- stabilize or destabilize the operation;
- reshape viability, cost, or participation;
- be necessary for continued UEF operation;
- damage, interrupt, or terminate the UEF.

None of those relations is sufficient for subject membership.

The distinction is:

```text
external process:
influences the owned frontier while retaining an independently
resolvable continuation in the relevant respect

constitutive process:
contributes to and bears the restriction resolved and inherited
through that owned frontier
```

An organismic process can therefore be external to the organism's UEF, while a technologically or environmentally located process could in principle become constitutive if its physical role entered the same owned operation. Ordinary labels such as organ, implant, interface, tool, or environment do not settle the relation.

## Embodiment

Closure must be physically instantiated. Energy dissipation, stress, damage, fatigue and finite recovery can condition actual operation, but those labels do not establish non-externalizable ownership or joint present organization. Embodiment names the physical realization and bearing of an independently qualifying restriction.

Embodiment does not independently define the subject:

- the body is not automatically the UEF;
- every bodily process is not automatically experiential;
- the body can support processes outside the UEF;
- an external interface can redistribute load without joining the UEF;
- nonbiological processes are not excluded in principle.

The relation among the terms is:

| Concept | Metaphysical role |
| --- | --- |
| Synchronic intrinsic closure | Intended joint present organization under intrinsic constraint; physical criterion incomplete |
| Ownership | The scope of the operation within which the frontier is borne and resolved |
| Bilateral participation | The process-relative condition of belonging to that ownership scope |
| Embodiment | How and where the burden and cost of closure are physically realized |

## Diachronic intrinsic closure

The UEF is not only unified at one frontier. It continues as the operation that produces its successor and inherits the consequences of its actual history.

The central recurrence is:

> Present topology constrains trajectory, and actual trajectory maintains or changes later topology.

The topology is not a second object that acts on the system. It is a description of the physical organization of admissible continuation. The trajectory is not an output detached from that organization. It is the actual succession through which the operation changes.

Closure under consequence means:

1. inherited organization constrains present continuation;
2. the extant operation lawfully produces its successor;
3. actual continuation maintains or changes the physical organization;
4. irreversible changes are welded, propagated, and sedimented where applicable;
5. the same continuing operation inherits the resulting conditions.

Input, output, and writing surface are therefore functional projections of one process. The UEF is the writing surface undergoing its own globally integrated rewriting.

[Closure under consequence](../../IER/IER-closure-under-consequence.md) explains
these related aspects of one operation; it adds no third closure test, universal
recurrence law or producer of experience. Generic feedback, copied records and
ancestry do not establish effective inherited consequences inside one continuing
operation. Earlier irreversible history can remain counterfactually effective
without a fresh collapse at every cut. Support membership, probability weights,
costs, margins and geometry are different objects.

## Numerical identity through change

The [continuity owner](../../IER/IER-continuity.md) defines `Adj_dia(a,b)`
for independently individuated adjacent nonzero-duration stages in a pre-fixed
physical comparison domain. All five conditions are required:

1. **Continuous owned closure:** joint present organization and internally borne closure
   spans the transition without a qualifying gap or incompatible independent
   resolution.
2. **Operational successor production:** actual dynamics produce the later
   regime-defining organization. External matter, energy, forcing and support
   are allowed; independent installation of a completed successor is not
   continuation.
3. **Typed consequence inheritance:** irreversible history-bearing changes
   remain physically effective and constrain later admissibility under relevant
   counterfactual variation. Fresh irreversible events are not needed at every
   pair, but no unmodelled gap may carry the identity claim.
4. **Operation-mediated change:** recruitment, release, turnover and boundary
   migration occur through the transition under constitutive membership.
5. **Unique two-sided continuation:** the later stage is the sole qualifying
   successor of the earlier stage under conditions 1–4, and the earlier stage
   is its sole qualifying predecessor in the fixed comparison domain.

A token is a maximal one-to-one chain connected by this relation without
intervening failure. Pointwise closure, no-gap operation and successor-only
uniqueness are individually insufficient.

Persistence does not require:

- identity of instantaneous state;
- unchanged experiential content;
- unchanged topology or participation distribution;
- retention of every material component;
- an unchanging anatomical boundary;
- memory, narrative, self-recognition, or personality continuity;
- a fresh collapse at every temporal advance.

Material turnover and boundary migration are compatible with identity when recruitment and release occur through the continuing operation and its consequences are inherited by it.

The same matter, organization type, or informational pattern is insufficient for numerical identity. What persists is the actual token process, not a description that could be copied or reinstantiated.

## Participation, content, binding, and importance

Within an intact UEF, participation can vary without altering subjecthood. Cognitive, sensory, interoceptive, autonomic, affective, motor, social, and other physical organizations may be recruited at different depths.

The following relations must remain separate:

| Concept | Question answered |
| --- | --- |
| Graded participation | How deeply is a process or topology recruited into lived organization? |
| Binding | Does a participating structure presently alter admissible futures? |
| Importance | Does a structure persistently, basin-integratedly, and dominantly organize continuation? |
| Constitutive participation | Does the physical process belong to the UEF's interval-level ownership scope? |

A constitutive process need not bind at every instant or appear important. A noncognitive bodily process can be attenuated while the UEF remains intact. Conversely, an external process can produce a bound or important structure inside the UEF without joining the subject.

Changes in participation alter experiential content and organization. They do not multiply, divide, enlarge, or diminish subjecthood while one UEF persists.

## Subject, self, and self-model

The experiential subject is the continuing UEF operation. It must not be conflated with structures organized within that operation.

| Term | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | The token UEF operation itself |
| Structural ownership | Non-externalizable bearing and resolution within that operation |
| Self/world organization | The lived distinction between owned burden and encountered external resistance |
| Self-model | A reproducible coordination basin stabilizing participation, bias, and frontier binding |
| Narrative self | A temporally organized compression within experience |

The self-model can fragment, reorganize, mislocate the body, incorporate tools phenomenologically, or exclude constitutive processes from awareness without changing the physical subject boundary.

Structural ownership persists even when ownership phenomenology, agency, access, or attribution is disrupted.

## Onset, persistence, and cessation

Subjecthood is categorical at the regime level.

### Onset

A subject begins when a qualifying UEF operation first becomes physically instantiated across a non-zero interval. Enabling variables may change gradually, but no subject exists before the regime exists.

### Persistence

The subject persists while the same token closure-bearing, successor-producing, consequence-inheriting operation continues. Content, participation, embodiment, and material membership may change within that continuity.

### Cessation

The subject token ends when any required stage-continuity condition fails.
A genuine UEF-null interval is sufficient but not necessary: identity can end
while another experiential operation remains present. Continued organism,
substrate, records, dispositions or enabling architecture do not preserve the
failed token.

### Reconstruction

Later recreation of the same state, organization, memories, topology, or functional capacities creates at most a numerically new operation. Similarity and causal reconstruction bridge neither a null interval nor any
other failed stage adjacency.

## Branching, fusion, nesting, and overlap

Each verdict depends on all five stage conditions, not resemblance or a
preferred narrative about which branch survives.

| Case | Conditional identity verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Asymmetric fission | One predecessor may persist through exactly one qualifying two-sided continuer; a separately closing branch begins a new token. Asymmetry alone is insufficient. |
| Symmetric fission | Multiple qualifying successors defeat uniqueness; the predecessor ends and later qualifying closures begin new tokens. |
| Fusion | Multiple qualifying predecessors defeat uniqueness; a merged operation cannot inherit both numerical identities. |
| Incorporation or expansion | One token may persist if it mediates membership change and retains the unique qualifying predecessor/successor relation while another ceases. |
| External installation | A completed successor installed independently fails operational production even if it resembles the earlier operation. |
| Reconstruction | A later qualifying operation after failed adjacency is new; copied state, memory or matter cannot restore the old token. |

Identity neither splits nor merges. These rules are conditional dispositions,
not empirical verdicts about actual people, brains or apparatus.

Physical nesting and overlap are not automatically fusion. One physical occurrence may occupy coherent roles in more than one scale-relative operation. Physical parthood does not imply phenomenal containment, content inheritance, shared access, or ownership of the same resolution.

What is prohibited is incompatible global ownership of the same resolution, not ordinary causal dependence, physical overlap, or multiscale organization.

## Immediate metaphysical consequences

If the identity claim is accepted, the following consequences are immediate:

1. **No additional subject bearer.** The subject is not something that has the UEF's experience.
2. **No experiential assembly.** One experience is not composed by summing local experiences or subjects.
3. **No cognitive threshold for subjecthood.** A UEF may be a noncognitive feeler rather than a thinker.
4. **No organism identity by default.** Organism and subject boundaries may differ.
5. **No anatomical privilege.** Biological interiority, neural location, and skin boundaries do not determine membership.
6. **No technological exclusion by default.** Physical role, not material origin, determines possible membership.
7. **No graded subjecthood.** Participation and capacities vary; existence as a subject does not.
8. **No transfer of ownership.** Experience cannot be passed, copied, reported, or externally completed as the same owned occurrence.
9. **No reconstruction survival.** A later duplicate is not the original token subject.
10. **No self-model criterion.** Felt mineness, alienness, embodiment, memory, narrative, and report do not define the physical boundary.
11. **No causal-isolation requirement.** A subject may be environmentally open while intrinsically closed.
12. **No automatic micropsychism.** Physical constituents need not be subjects or experiences merely because the global operation is.
13. **No automatic collective subject.** Coordination, communication, institutional persistence, or shared goals do not establish one owned frontier.
14. **No epistemic authority from experience.** Intrinsic reality does not reveal mind-independent truth, ontology, morality, or correctness.

These are consequences of the proposed identity and boundary relations. They are not independent criteria for detecting a UEF.

## What is complete and what remains open

This exposition states an identity commitment, retained physical relations and
conditional consequences. The replacement physical unity formulation remains
incomplete; a completed historical review does not close that question.
Conditional on a UEF, it states:

- what the experience is;
- what the subject is;
- why experiential unity follows from identity with that qualifying operation;
- which physical relations constrain the subject boundary;
- what makes a process a constituent;
- how causal openness is compatible with intrinsic closure;
- how embodiment relates to ownership;
- what preserves numerical identity through change;
- what onset, cessation, reconstruction, branching, and overlap mean; and
- what follows immediately from those commitments.

Remaining work follows the theoretical burdens and scope boundary
(`_work/theoretical-burdens.md` in the development repository):

- physically specify constitutive continuation domains and reconcile qualification,
  closure, ownership, membership and continuity without circular assumptions;
- explain how activity produces or maintains constitutive organization, and
  compare its scope with supplied conditions and interacting organizations;
- test bounded structural claims and record supported, adverse and underdetermined
  results, including whether any additional unity condition is justified;
- develop defeasible empirical and abductive warrant for particular identifications,
  including comparison with constitution and realization;
- investigate particular and comparative qualitative identifications as two
  descriptions of the same occurrence, subject to structural privacy; and
- synchronize affected expositions and publications at their declared source
  baselines while preserving completed reviews at their recorded bounds.

This programme does not owe a mechanism producing experience from a completed
nonexperiential operation, a bond assembling antecedent experiences, an
infallible detector, or a universal catalogue decoding physical organization
into phenomenal labels. It does not promise an exact boundary algorithm for
every possible candidate. Bounded physical specification and defeasible
identification remain substantive work; evidence may leave a verdict
underdetermined. These limits do not protect the proposal from contradictions,
systematic scope failures or reliable mismatches between independently
described physical and experiential structure.

The identity case is a posteriori and abductive: independently specified
physical organization, proposed token coextension and ontological economy
support a defeasible inference, not a deduction from structural equations.
Schema articulation, physical typing, empirical attribution, qualitative
individuation and identity warrant are distinct burdens. Qualitative character
is the occurrence of the relevant intrinsic organization under the proposal,
not an additional product that the organization must generate. The accepted S2
waterfall dossier demonstrates an idealized transport/turnover subcase;
ownership, full membership, inherited consequences and two-sided adjacency
remain unestablished there. It is neither a whole-stack instantiation nor an
experiential attribution. Wider propagation remains a governed revision task.

## Guidance for subsequent theory work

Subsequent theory work in this programme should preserve the following order:

```text
candidate physical organization and persistence through change
-> constitutive scope, owned closure and membership
-> full successor production, inheritance and token continuity
-> qualifying operation under the UEF commitments
-> identity with total experience and subject
-> experiential unity and differentiated content
```

This is an explanatory order, not a temporal sequence in which a completed
nonexperiential operation later acquires experience. Physical relations can
constrain one another and require joint assessment. Numerical token continuity
cannot establish its own closure or membership premises. The proposal assigns
constitutive scope and ownership boundary to the conditions qualifying and
individuating the operation. Their physical adequacy remains open; no missing
phenomenal assembly mechanism is implied.

The ordering therefore carries unity's physical work into those preceding
conditions. An exposition must retain the reasoning about activity,
organizational maintenance, joint scope, owned resolution and produced and
inherited continuation. A later sentence saying that unity follows under
identity cannot substitute for that reasoning.

It should not reverse that order by deriving the subject from cognition, report, organism membership, self-model, importance, behaviour, or familiar anatomy.

New explanations should identify which level they concern:

- operation existence;
- subject identity;
- boundary membership;
- momentary participation;
- frontier binding;
- geometrical deformation;
- collapse and inheritance;
- embodiment;
- cognitive or phenomenological organization;
- epistemic evidence.

No downstream article should use an effect at one level as an unargued criterion at another.

## Final compression

> One qualifying continuing physical operation is the total experience and its subject. The identity proposal requires no additional experiential product or bearer; its unity account requires no assembly of antecedent experiences. Contents differentiate that same occurrence. Unity's physical work concerns the activity, maintained organization, joint scope, owned resolution and produced and inherited continuation through which the operation is qualified and individuated. The physical proposal is that these conditions also determine its constitutive scope and ownership boundary. The positive task is to specify and test those conditions and develop defeasible warrant for the identification. Their physical adequacy remains open. Physical incompleteness does not reinstate a production bridge, phenomenal bond, detector or universal qualitative decoder.
