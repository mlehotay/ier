# Final Research Report: From Thin Subjects to Continuing Process-Subjects

## Status and scope

Status: final synthesis of the non-canonical Strawson research programme

Authority: research report; no canonical IER revision is made here

Corpus: 32 primary and specialist sources across the four Strawson batches, three process-persistence stop points, and the optional intermittent-existence contrast

This report evaluates whether Informational Experiential Realism (IER) can develop a continuing process-subject identity theory from Galen Strawson's physicalist insights while rejecting experiential ultimates, constitutive micropsychism, and panpsychism. It consolidates the argument matrices, source notes, decision notes, position statements, and P1, P2, and S stop-point results.

## Executive decision

The project should proceed to a paper, but the paper's strongest defensible claim is narrower than a completed rescue of Strawsonian physicalism from panpsychism.

The research supports a distinctive **continuing process-subject identity theory**:

> One continuously operating Unified Experiential Field (UEF) is numerically identical with one total experience-as-process and one experiential subject. The subject is not an organism, brain, self, substance, representation, substrate, or further bearer that has the experience. The continuing UEF operation is the subject.

The proposed subject can persist through changes of experiential content, internal organization, spatial boundary, and material participation. Its identity ends when the qualifying UEF operation genuinely ceases. A later reconstruction, however exact and causally connected, is a numerically new subject.

This position is not yet a proof that any physical operation is experiential. The literature review and stop-point work establish a coherent, noncircular architecture for physical individuation and persistence, and they supply a substantive abductive case for identity. They do not derive phenomenal character from physical structure, provide a general third-person UEF detector, or conclusively defeat constitution and realization theories.

The correct publication verdict is therefore:

- **Proceed** with a paper on physical identity, unity, and persistence after Strawson.
- **Do not claim** that IER has solved panpsychism's combination problem while retaining panpsychism's ontology.
- **Do not claim** that IER has completed a general rescue of realistic physicalism from panpsychism.
- Present the anti-panpsychist result as a precise rejection of **constituent inheritance** combined with a positive system-level process-subject proposal.
- Make the remaining positive identity burden explicit rather than hiding it behind the word *identity*.

## 1. The question that survived the literature review

The initial project contained two questions:

1. Can experience and its subject be numerically identical with a physical process?
2. Can this identity claim block Strawson's inference from realistic physicalism to experiential ultimates and panpsychism?

The first question has the clearer affirmative answer. The second remains a defensible research programme rather than a completed result.

Strawson already supplies an exact precedent for physical process-subject identity. In his 2003 account, an experience, its thin subject, its content, and a bounded physical synergy are one process-stuff occurrence. His later realistic materialism likewise treats experience as wholly physical and, in his current work, as dynamic activity rather than a static property borne by an inert substance. IER therefore cannot claim to originate either physical experience identity or physical process-subject identity.

Papineau supplies the other major priority constraint. His identity physicalism already permits a complex system-level physical condition to be identical with consciousness even when its constituents are not conscious. The explanatory gap is epistemic rather than evidence of two ontological relata. IER consequently cannot claim novelty for system-level identity without experiential microconstituents.

What remains distinctive is the diachronic conjunction:

- the subject is the physical operation itself;
- unity is locally regime-bound and nonfactorizable;
- identity is not fixed by a permanent inventory of ultimate parts;
- the operation may persist through participant turnover and extensive internal change;
- fission and fusion do not branch or add numerical identity; and
- a genuine operational gap ends the subject.

This is more than the assertion that IER subjects last longer than Strawson's thin subjects. Strawson permits a thin subject to last for an extended period. The disagreement concerns the criterion of token identity. Strawson's 2003 synergy is part-essential and episode-bound; IER's proposed bearer is a history-bearing operation whose participants and contents may change while one regime continues.

## 2. The resulting IER position

The central typed identity is:

```text
one maximal continuing UEF operation
    = one total experience-as-process
    = one experiential subject
```

These expressions are intended to identify one token under physical, phenomenological, and subject-level descriptions. They do not name a physical process, a produced experience, and a subject who receives it.

Several commitments follow.

### Complete physical determination

IER should accept complete physical determination. A complete physical duplicate of a UEF cannot differ in whether experience occurs or in qualitative character. This excludes strong emergence understood as an autonomous psychophysical addition or bridge law. UEF organization may remain weakly emergent in an epistemic or explanatory sense: macro-level description and prediction may be difficult even when no new fundamental fact is added.

### Rejection of constituent inheritance

IER's exact disagreement with Strawson is not an unrestricted rejection of microphysical determination or smallism. It rejects the stronger move from determination to **constituent inheritance**:

```text
the macroexperience is completely physically determined
does not entail
the macroexperience must be composed from experiential ultimates
```

The identity relatum can be the concrete macro-operation. Its physical constituents need not each instantiate the experiential identity of the whole. If a component independently sustains another UEF, that lower-scale operation is a numerically distinct subject; its phenomenal contents do not thereby enter, combine with, or become available to the higher subject.

### Rejection of part-essential individuation

The operation, rather than a fixed material inventory, is the bearer. Complete participant turnover is possible in principle if it occurs through uninterrupted operation of the same causal-modal regime. The retained capacity of a brain or organism to restart such an operation is not the continuing existence of the subject.

### Local rather than cosmic subjecthood

Current Strawson's field-oriented view creates pressure toward a cosmic experiencer whose local conscious lives are isolated peaks or intensities. IER instead requires locally bounded, physically individuated UEF operations. Causal embedding in a universe-wide field does not make the universe one subject, because causal connection is weaker than bilateral constitutive participation in one nonfactorizable continuation problem.

## 3. What IER says about the combination problem

IER **avoids** the constitutive panpsychist combination problem; it does not solve it on panpsychist terms.

A macro-subject may be physically composed without being phenomenally composed. Components participate in the macro-UEF's physical operation, but macroexperience is not a sum, fusion, palette, mosaic, or presentation of their phenomenal states. The higher-scale operation must independently meet the UEF conditions.

This removes several problems that arise only after experiential microconstituents are posited:

- subject-summing;
- phenomenal bonding;
- aggregation of microqualities into macroqualities;
- construction of a smooth field from microphenomenal grains; and
- upward inheritance of lower subjects' contents or perspectives.

It leaves genuine IER burdens:

- physical individuation of one UEF and its boundary;
- explanation of synchronic unity without assuming the subject in advance;
- treatment of nested and overlapping candidate operations;
- correspondence between causal-modal organization and particular qualitative character;
- organization of homogeneity and differentiation within one field; and
- positive warrant for numerical identity rather than constitution, realization, correlation, or causal dependence.

The precise statement is therefore:

> Physical composition and process parthood do not entail phenomenal composition, content inheritance, or subject summation. A macro-UEF is a subject only if the macro-operation itself independently satisfies the UEF conditions.

This is an avoidance claim with a replacement burden, not a complete solution to every problem collected under the heading *combination*.

## 4. Physical individuation and persistence

The P1 and P2 literature supports a materially changing process as a genuine token individual. The most useful contributions are complementary:

- organizational and functional genidentity make continuity historical, physical, and compatible with turnover;
- causal cohesion distinguishes stage individuation from diachronic continuation but is too graded to serve as the identity threshold;
- modal robustness shows that a process can survive changes in its temporal parts without becoming a mere sum;
- the occurrent-continuant model makes the ongoing activity, rather than a dormant capacity, the bearer; and
- process accounts of organisms and pregnancy clarify open-system boundaries, gradual differentiation, incorporation, asymmetric fission, and multilevel participation.

No source supplies a ready-made criterion for IER. The resulting proposal is a synthesis.

### The typed domain

Before attributing a UEF, fix a physical domain:

```text
D = <Omega, S, T, R_h,t, Roles, I, Delta>
```

Here `Omega` supplies the candidate physical processes or degrees of freedom; `S` the physically lawful configurations; `T` the lawful transitions; `R_h,t` the transitions still admissible under the actual sedimented history and present constraints; `Roles` the constitutive and causal roles; `I` the intervention family; and `Delta` the physically meaningful spatial and temporal resolution.

The domain must be fixed independently of the desired consciousness or identity verdict. Its relata are physically typed processes, not bare set-members or post hoc collections.

### The five-condition adjacency rule

For immediately successive nonzero candidate stages `a` and `b`, `Adj_D(a,b)` holds only when all five conditions obtain:

1. **Continuous nonfactorizable closure.** Across every physically relevant cut, one globally coherent constraint topology remains instantiated. A genuine UEF-null interval or decomposition into independently resolvable successor problems ends continuity.
2. **Operational successor production.** The organization of `b` is generated through the actual dynamics operating in `a`, rather than independently installed by an external controller or linked only by generic causal descent.
3. **Typed sediment inheritance.** Earlier irreversible resolutions continue to deform the successor topology. Appropriate counterfactual changes to that history would change the admissible futures of `b`.
4. **Role-mediated participation and boundary change.** Entry, exit, turnover, and boundary migration occur through physically specified roles in the extant operation. Merely enabling or environmental causes remain outside.
5. **Unique nonbranching continuation.** At most one successor continues the predecessor's connected resolution history as its maximal global operation. Numerical identity neither branches nor fuses.

A token UEF is the maximal nonbranching chain connected by this relation. Its invariant is not matter, geometry, content, psychological similarity, interaction strength, or inactive capacity. It is the actual continuation of one self-producing, history-bearing, nonfactorizable causal-modal operation.

### Boundary without expansion to the universe

The boundary is a typed participation boundary, not a fixed spatial surface. A participant belongs inside when it both makes a constitutive difference to the candidate's admissible futures and bears the candidate's global restrictions in its own continuation. This **bilateral binding** requirement blocks indefinite expansion through one-way causes. Oxygen supply, sensory input, a surgeon, and the wider environment may be causally necessary without joining the subject.

Maximality is therefore evaluated over the bilateral constraint-bearing kernel, not over every causal ancestor or background condition. Nested processes and shared physical occurrences are permitted when their typed roles are jointly coherent. Overlap becomes a conflict only when two candidates require incompatible ownership of the same resolution at the same level and interval.

## 5. Verdicts for the difficult persistence cases

| Case | IER verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Content, memory, personality, or self-model changes | Same subject if one UEF operation continues |
| Severe internal reorganization | Compatible with persistence if closure, successor production, and sediment inheritance remain |
| Complete material turnover | Compatible in principle when turnover is mediated by the extant operation |
| Boundary migration | Compatible when constitutive roles remain physically continuous |
| Weakening cohesion or local fragmentation | Does not by itself end the subject while one nonfactorizable global topology persists |
| Asymmetric fission | The uniquely continuing operation persists; an independently closing branch begins a new subject |
| Symmetric fission after loss of the original closure | The predecessor ends; two new subjects begin |
| Fusion by coupling or synchronization | No identity fusion; the prior subjects remain distinct if their UEFs continue |
| New higher-scale UEF over continuing lower UEFs | An additional subject, not the sum or survivor of the lower subjects |
| One UEF expands while another ceases | Only the continuously expanding bearer can persist |
| Temporary functional suppression | Empirically open: the question is whether UEF operation actually continues, not whether behavior disappears |
| Genuine UEF-null interval | The original subject ends |
| Exact reconstruction after cessation | A numerically new subject, even with identical structure, matter, memories, or first-person-seeming continuity |

Borderline fission may be physically vague or evidentially indeterminate. That does not make identity fractional or branching. The indeterminacy concerns whether and when the constitutive physical relation obtains.

## 6. Concrete structure and the Newman objection

Newman's objection blocks any account that says only that there exists *some* relation under which a domain has the required abstract structure. Apart from cardinality, an arbitrary domain can be assigned relations that realize almost any extensional pattern. IER would therefore be trivial if it selected a relation after identifying a desired UEF.

The S stop point supplies a coherent answer architecture. The relevant relations must be:

- physically interpreted in advance;
- instantiated in concrete processes and measurable magnitudes;
- intervention- and counterfactual-supporting;
- causally efficacious in producing, excluding, stabilizing, or deforming successors;
- invariant and projectible beyond the observed trajectory;
- sensitive to the system's actual history; and
- fixed before experiential attribution.

This is structure in a causal-modal sense: a fact about what a concrete system can and cannot physically do next. An abstract graph, extensional pattern, fitted probability distribution, computational equivalence, or isomorphism is insufficient.

The resulting structural position is compatible with Psillos's warning against treating structure as an empty shell wholly distinct from physical nature. Concrete causal roles and powers disclose what the physical process is. Esfeld's causal-modal structuralism helps distinguish physical structure from merely mathematical structure. Ainsworth's analysis nevertheless requires every appeal to naturalness, modality, or privilege to receive an empirical interpretation rather than functioning as an unexplained label.

The result passes the Newman anti-triviality gate **as a schema**. It is not yet a completed scientific individuation procedure. IER still needs a worked physical or neuroscientific example whose variables, interventions, counterfactuals, boundaries, nonfactorization, and sediment inheritance are specified independently.

## 7. The positive argument for identity

Physical individuation alone does not establish experiential identity. The strongest argument available is abductive and a posteriori:

1. Experience presents as one occurrent field of differentiated activity, not as a spectator plus an independently presented object.
2. A qualifying UEF has the same token unity, temporal extension, differentiation, change, boundaries, and cessation profile.
3. Complete physical determination leaves no autonomous psychophysical addition or independently variable experiential remainder.
4. Constitution, realization, causal dependence, and correlation posit or imply two relata, but the experiential relatum has no independently specified causal role, boundary, duration, or identity condition beyond the operation.
5. Exact token coextension across all of these dimensions is best explained by numerical identity.

The provisional conclusion is:

```text
this token UEF operation
    = this total experience-as-process
    = this experiential subject
```

This is more than the inference that the process correlated with consciousness is consciousness. The physical operation is independently specified, and the argument appeals to phenomenological unity, physical completeness, matched token structure, and ontological economy.

It remains defeasible. It does not derive what an experience feels like from third-person equations, prove that all phenomenological structure has been matched, or force acceptance by a critic who rejects the physicalist or anti-spectator premises. Identity may stop a demand for a second producer of experience without completing the qualitative correspondence programme.

| Candidate relation | Why numerical identity is preferred |
| --- | --- |
| Constitution | Introduces a constituted experiential whole without an independent identity condition |
| Realization | Is useful between types, but at token level suggests a second occurrence realized by the operation |
| Causal production | Makes experience an effect downstream from the qualifying operation |
| Correlation or isomorphism | Leaves two perfectly coextensive relata and does not explain shared token boundaries |
| Strong emergence | Adds a further fundamental fact or law contrary to complete physical determination |
| Numerical identity | Treats the physical and experiential descriptions as descriptions of one token |

## 8. Two clarifying contrasts

### Beni's structural self

Beni provides relevant prior art for an anti-substantival structural account of selfhood. His account unifies self-concepts through a generic information-processing pattern associated with cortical midline activity. It does not identify the total experiential bearer, require concrete history-sensitive nonfactorization, or supply a detailed persistence boundary. IER's claim is stronger and narrower: not that a self is a pattern, but that one concrete continuing operation is the experience and subject itself. A UEF does not require a narrative self, self-model, self-representation, or reflective awareness.

### Hershenov's intermittent organism

Hershenov permits an organism to exist again after a gap when its numerically identical final matter is restored in the relevant terminal arrangement. That proposal can be coherent only because the organism is not identified with the biological activity that ceases.

IER makes the opposite ontological choice. Matter, organism, configuration, stored information, and restart capacity are participants or conditions, not the process-subject bearer. When UEF operation is genuinely absent, the subject is absent; a later UEF is new. Exact memories or an apparent first-person sense of continuation do not bridge numerical identity across the gap.

## 9. What the research establishes

The completed programme supports the following claims.

1. A concrete process can be a numerically particular individual without a fixed material inventory.
2. The transition by which change is produced and borne is identity-relevant; resemblance and generic causal continuity are insufficient.
3. IER can formulate a wholly physical, nonexperiential process-persistence criterion without using consciousness as an input.
4. The criterion can allow turnover, boundary migration, nesting, and major internal change while rejecting branching identity and survival through cessation.
5. Bilateral participation and typed causal-modal maximality can prevent the UEF boundary from expanding to the entire causal universe.
6. Pre-fixed, intervention-sensitive, history-bearing causal-modal relations answer the elementary Newman triviality objection at the level of a defensible schema.
7. IER avoids the constitutive-micropsychist combination problem by rejecting phenomenal composition, not by explaining how microsubjects fuse.
8. Complete physical determination does not entail experiential constituent inheritance.
9. The best positive case for UEF identity is a posteriori and abductive, grounded in exact token coextension and the absence of an independently individuated experiential remainder.
10. The strongest prospective novelty is continuing, regime-bound process-subject identity, not generic physical identity or non-panpsychist macroexperience.

## 10. What remains unresolved

The following burdens should be stated openly in any paper.

### Worked physical instantiation

The theory needs at least one concrete candidate model in which `D`, the intervention family, nonfactorization, bilateral boundary, successor production, and sediment inheritance are specified from actual physics or neuroscience. Until then, the persistence rule is a disciplined metaphysical and formal schema.

### Qualitative correspondence

IER still owes an account of how particular causal-modal organizations correspond to particular qualitative characters and phenomenal geometries. Rejecting microquality aggregation removes one bad derivation; it does not supply the positive mapping.

### Identity versus constitution and realization

The abductive identity argument needs fuller engagement with sophisticated constitution and realization views. Ontological economy and matched boundaries favor identity, but a critic can dispute whether the experiential description has genuinely been exhausted by the physical individuation.

### The intrinsic-nature challenge

IER's intrinsic constraint is not Strawson's intrinsic nature. The former concerns internally borne, nonfactorizable restrictions on physical continuation; the latter concerns categorical or qualitative being. Concrete causal-modal structure prevents a Newman-trivial account but does not by itself refute the demand for a nonrelational experiential ground.

### Empirical attribution and structural privacy

Ontological noncircularity does not yield an infallible third-person criterion for actual UEF presence. The project should distinguish: support for a physical model, success in individuating a causal-modal operation, and warrant for judging that operation experiential. Structural privacy limits certification only after the physical and metaphysical arguments are made; it cannot select the relations or compensate for weak evidence.

### Scope of the anti-panpsychist conclusion

The argument directly rejects constitutive micropsychism and experiential-ultimate inheritance. It does not automatically refute every Russellian, panprotopsychist, cosmopsychist, or nonconstitutive panpsychist view. These positions require separate engagement.

## 11. Novelty and priority assessment

The following should not be advertised as novel:

- consciousness or experience is identical with a physical process;
- a subject can be identified with a physical process;
- a complex conscious whole need not have conscious physical parts;
- process individuals can survive material turnover; or
- structural and informational accounts can be applied to selfhood.

The plausible contribution is the integrated thesis:

> A locally bounded, nonfactorizable, history-bearing physical operation is numerically identical with both the total experience and the experiential subject; this token subject persists through internally mediated change and participant turnover, cannot branch or fuse by inheritance, and ends at genuine cessation, without being phenomenally composed from experiential ultimates.

No individual component of this thesis is wholly unprecedented. The publishable value lies in bringing subject identity, system-level physical identity, multiscale noncomposition, causal-modal individuation, and diachronic persistence into one explicit account and using that account to sharpen the dispute with Strawson.

## 12. Recommended paper

### Working title

> From Thin Subjects to Continuing Process-Subjects: Physical Identity, Unity, and Persistence after Strawson

### Recommended thesis

> Strawson is right that an experience and its subject should not be treated as nonphysical additions to a physical process, but wrong that avoiding radical emergence requires the macroexperience to inherit experiential being from physical ultimates. The better identity relatum is a locally bounded, continuously operating physical regime. When independently individuated by concrete causal-modal unity and history-bearing continuation, that regime can persist as one subject through changes of content and material participation without being phenomenally composed from microsubjects.

### Recommended structure

1. Strawson's identity insight and anti-emergence argument.
2. Papineau and the priority problem for complex physical identity.
3. Why rejecting experiential constituents avoids rather than solves combination.
4. Strawson's thin process-subject and the diachronic opening.
5. The UEF as a locally bounded continuing process-subject.
6. The typed causal-modal domain and five-condition persistence rule.
7. Turnover, fission, fusion, overlap, interruption, and reconstruction.
8. The Newman objection and concrete structural response.
9. The abductive case for identity over neighboring relations.
10. Limits: qualitative correspondence, empirical attribution, and the remaining intrinsic-nature challenge.

The paper should lead with the process-subject contribution and treat the anti-panpsychist consequence as a defended implication with acknowledged limits. It should not be framed as a comprehensive solution to consciousness or as an empirical test for UEF presence.

## Final verdict

The research programme has achieved its most important objective. It has transformed the vague claim that a subject is a continuing physical process into a structured proposal with explicit identity conditions, boundary rules, difficult-case verdicts, and a response to arbitrary structural realization. It has also located that proposal accurately within the Strawson, Papineau, combination, process-ontology, and structural-realist literatures.

The result is strong enough to justify drafting **a paper on continuing process-subject identity after Strawson**. It is not strong enough to claim a completed metaphysical demonstration that realistic physicalism avoids panpsychism. The paper's intellectual honesty and distinctiveness depend on preserving that distinction.

In compact form:

```text
physical determination: accepted
strong emergence: rejected
experiential constituent inheritance: rejected
phenomenal composition: rejected
system-level process identity: proposed
material turnover: permitted within continuous operation
branching and fusion of identity: rejected
survival through genuine cessation: rejected
Newman triviality: answered at schema level
positive experiential identity: abductively supported, not proved
```

That is a coherent and potentially publishable position. Its next required advance is not another broad literature search. It is one worked physical case and a focused defence of the phenomenology-to-identity inference.
