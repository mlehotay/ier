## 1. The question that survived the literature review

The initial project contained two questions:

1. Can experience and its subject be numerically identical with a physical process?
2. Can this identity claim block Strawson's inference from realistic physicalism to experiential ultimates and panpsychism?

The first question has the clearer affirmative answer. The second remains a defensible research programme rather than a completed result.

Strawson already supplies an exact precedent for physical process-subject identity. In his 2003 account, an experience, its thin subject, its content, and a bounded physical synergy are one process-stuff occurrence. His later realistic materialism likewise treats experience as wholly physical and, in his current work, as dynamic activity rather than a static property borne by an inert substance. IER therefore cannot claim to originate either physical experience identity or physical process-subject identity.

Papineau supplies the other major priority constraint. His identity physicalism already permits a complex system-level physical condition to be identical with consciousness even when its constituents are not conscious. The explanatory gap is epistemic rather than evidence of two ontological relata. IER consequently cannot claim novelty for system-level identity without experiential microconstituents.

The particular continuing-subject account developed here connects the following
relations. This comparison describes the account without claiming historical
novelty for their conjunction:

- the subject is the physical operation itself;
- unity is locally regime-bound and synchronically nonfactorizable;
- successor organization is operationally produced and self-inherits typed consequences;
- identity is not fixed by a permanent inventory of ultimate parts;
- the operation may persist through participant turnover and extensive internal change;
- fission and fusion do not branch or add numerical identity; and
- failure of the full continuity relation ends the token, even without a system-wide experiential gap.

This is more than the assertion that IER subjects last longer than Strawson's thin subjects. Strawson permits a thin subject to last for an extended period. The disagreement concerns the criterion of token identity. Strawson's 2003 synergy is part-essential and episode-bound; IER's proposed bearer is a history-bearing operation whose participants and contents may change while one regime continues.

## 2. The resulting IER position

The central typed identity is:

```text
one continuing UEF operation at its maximal compatible
ownership/participation scope and interval
    = one total experience-as-process
    = one experiential subject
```

These expressions are intended to identify one token under physical, phenomenological, and subject-level descriptions. They do not name a physical process, a produced experience, and a subject who receives it.

Several commitments follow.

### Complete physical determination

IER should accept complete physical determination. A complete physical duplicate of a UEF, including the relevant constitutive history, boundary, and dynamics, cannot differ in whether experience occurs or in qualitative character. This excludes strong emergence understood as an autonomous psychophysical addition or bridge law. UEF organization may remain weakly emergent in an epistemic or explanatory sense: macro-level description and prediction may be difficult even when no new fundamental fact is added.

### Rejection of constituent inheritance

IER's exact disagreement with Strawson is not an unrestricted rejection of microphysical determination or smallism. It rejects the stronger move from determination to **constituent inheritance**:

```text
the macroexperience is completely physically determined
does not entail
the macroexperience must be composed from experiential ultimates
```

The identity relatum can be the concrete operation. Participating processes
contribute physical differences without supplying phenomenal contents that
must be summed or transmitted. The argument makes no assumption about
component-level experiential status.

### Rejection of part-essential individuation

The operation, rather than a fixed material inventory, is the bearer. Complete participant turnover is possible in principle if it is mediated by operation satisfying the full accepted continuity relation. The retained capacity of a brain or organism to restart such an operation is not the continuing existence of the subject.

### Local rather than cosmic subjecthood

Current Strawson's field-oriented view creates pressure toward a cosmic experiencer whose local conscious lives are isolated peaks or intensities. IER instead requires locally bounded, physically individuated UEF operations. Causal embedding in a universe-wide field does not make the universe one subject, because causal connection is weaker than bilateral constitutive participation in one nonfactorizable continuation problem.

## 4. Physical Individuation and Persistence

The P1/P2 sources supply complementary process-individuation and persistence
resources. Their historical claims remain in the source notes and comparison
matrices. No source supplies a ready-made IER criterion; the current account
is the accepted synthesis, constrained by those precedents.

### Physical Declarations and Dependency Order

Before attribution, declare the concrete candidate, variables, lawful
transition structure, grain, frontier, interval, relevant history, physically
meaningful partitions, interventions, restrictions, and comparison needed by
the bounded question. Use local notation only where it operates on that
question. There is no universal domain tuple or mandatory verification envelope.

The order is:

```text
physical candidate
  -> synchronic intrinsic closure
  -> independently individuated ownership scope
  -> constitutive membership
  -> diachronic continuity
  -> conditional experiential interpretation
```

No downstream identity or phenomenological verdict selects an earlier
physical boundary or supplies evidence for it.

### Synchronic Closure and Constitutive Membership

`IER-intrinsic-closure.md` fixes outgoing admissible support `A(s)` and
common-fibre projections at the same scope. Closure requires nonfactorization
across every partition in the exhaustive predeclared family of physically
meaningful nontrivial partitions. Dependence across one chosen partition,
statistical nonfactorization, or a coupled trajectory is insufficient.

Ownership is independently individuated by non-externalizable bearing of
the frontier burden. `IER-processes.md` requires five jointly necessary
membership relations: contribution, global bearing, joint nonexternalizable
resolution, diachronic uptake, and common typing.

Reciprocal causal arrows alone are insufficient. This replaces the earlier
compressed bilateral-binding recipe as current exposition; it supplies no
general kernel-finding algorithm. The maximal compatible participation scope
is not the entire causal ancestry of the system.

### Core Diachronic Closure

For adjacent nonzero-duration physical stages `a` and `b`,
`IER-continuity.md` owns `Adj_dia(a,b)`. Its first three conditions supply
the core diachronic closure:

1. **Continuous owned closure:** globally nonfactorizable, internally borne
   closure spans the relevant cuts without a qualifying gap or incompatible
   independently resolved successor operations.
2. **Operational successor production:** actual dynamics at `a` produce the
   regime-defining organization at `b`. External matter, energy, forcing, and
   support are permitted; independent installation of a completed successor
   is not continuation.
3. **Typed consequence inheritance:** physically irreversible history-bearing
   changes remain effective through the transition and constrain later
   admissibility. Counterfactual variation matters. Copied records,
   resemblance, generic state dependence, and dormant capacity do not suffice.
   No fresh irreversible episode is needed at every pair; inheritance must be
   physically supported densely enough at the chosen grain to leave no
   unmodelled identity-bearing gap.

### Boundary and Numerical-Identity Discipline

The remaining two conditions are equally required:

4. **Operation-mediated change:** recruitment, release, turnover, geometry
   change, and boundary migration occur through the transition under the
   constitutive-membership relation. Independent incompatible ownership
   cannot be described as internal reorganization.
5. **Unique two-sided continuation:** within the pre-fixed physical comparison
   domain, `b` is the sole qualifying successor of `a` and `a` is the sole
   qualifying predecessor of `b` under conditions 1–4.

The grouping explains the rule; it neither changes its five conditions nor
makes boundary and uniqueness optional. A token is a maximal one-to-one chain.
A successor-only test fails at fusion. Matter, content, geometric resemblance,
and preserved capacity are not numerical identity invariants.

A UEF-null interval is sufficient for cessation, not necessary. Production,
inheritance, mediated membership change, or two-sided uniqueness can fail while
some experiential operation remains present.

### Topology–Trajectory Recurrence and the Pipeline

Topology describes organized admissibility; trajectory describes realized
continuation. Physical restrictions shape possible traversal. Actual
continuation produces, maintains, and inherits organization that shapes later
admissibility. Recurrence is explanatory, not a universal mathematical law,
a second topology, or a sufficient condition for experience.

[`IER-pipeline.md`](../../IER/IER-pipeline.md) preserves reproduction, participation, binding,
soft bonding, curvature, collapse, welding, propagation, and sedimentation as
typed roles. This order is a conditional irreversible episode within ongoing
operation, not a compulsory event at each advance.

Collapse irreversibly contracts reachability membership. Changes in cost,
margin, or geometry can preserve reachability support. Welding incorporates
a collapse consequence; propagation redistributes it; sedimentation stabilizes
history that remains physically effective in later continuation. Re-entry
engages inherited organization without replaying stored experiential frames.

Closure under consequence connects these owners without adding a third
criterion. Structural closure terminates internal explanation at the
continuing operation rather than at repeated collapse or an observer.

### Boundaries, Openness, and Overlap

A cause is not excluded merely for being external to an anatomical envelope,
nor included because it is indispensable. Its typed role must satisfy the
full membership relation. Open material and energy exchange can support
continuation without installing a completed organization.

Nested and overlapping operations require independently declared scopes.
Shared matter or a participant occurrence is not a shared token stage.
Dominance conflict concerns incompatible ownership of the same resolution,
participating organization, and interval; physical overlap alone is insufficient.

## 5. Continuity Verdicts for Difficult Cases

Every persistence verdict below requires all five `Adj_dia` conditions,
including unique predecessor and successor within the declared comparison.
The table gives conditional dispositions, not an empirical classification of
any actual organism or apparatus.

| Case | Continuity disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Content, memory, personality, or self-model changes | Can preserve the token when the full relation holds; resemblance is neither necessary nor sufficient |
| Severe reorganization or weakening cohesion | Can preserve identity if owned closure and all other conditions remain; cohesion alone is not the threshold |
| Complete turnover or boundary migration | Can preserve identity when the operation mediates the change and retains the full relation |
| Local fragmentation | Does not multiply subjects while the same owned global operation and full stage relation persist |
| Asymmetric fission | Exactly one unique two-sided continuer can preserve the predecessor; an independently closing branch begins a new token |
| Symmetric fission | Without a unique successor, the predecessor token ends and later qualifying closures begin new tokens, even without a UEF-null interval |
| Coupling or synchronization | Does not merge identity; each prior token persists only if its own full relation continues |
| New higher-scale closure over continuing lower operations | Requires independent individuation; it cannot inherit the numerical identity of several predecessors |
| Incorporation or expansion while another token ceases | One token can persist only with operation-mediated membership change and a unique qualifying predecessor and successor; asymmetry alone is insufficient |
| Fusion with two qualifying predecessors | Unique-predecessor condition fails; no merged survivor of both earlier tokens |
| Physical overlap | Not shared identity; independently typed scopes and coherent ownership are required |
| Functional suppression, sleep, anesthesia, or unresponsiveness | Labels do not establish either persistence or cessation; determine whether the full physical relation remains supported |
| Genuine UEF-null interval | Sufficient to end the token; substrate and capacity may persist |
| Production, inheritance, membership mediation, or uniqueness failure | Can end the token without a system-wide experiential gap |
| Exact reconstruction or replay after cessation | New token if a qualifying operation begins; copied matter, memory, records, or apparent first-person continuity do not bridge the failure |

Borderline or evidentially indeterminate cases remain questions about whether
the physical relation obtains. They do not justify fractional, branching,
merged, or resemblance-based numerical identity.

## 6. Concrete structure and the Newman objection

Newman's objection blocks any account that says only that there exists *some* relation under which a domain has the required abstract structure. Apart from cardinality, an arbitrary domain can be assigned relations that realize almost any extensional pattern. IER would therefore be trivial if it selected a relation after identifying a desired UEF.

The S stop point supplies a coherent answer architecture. The relevant relations must be:

- physically interpreted in advance;
- instantiated in concrete processes and measurable magnitudes;
- intervention- and counterfactual-supporting;
- causally efficacious in producing, excluding, stabilizing, or deforming successors;
- invariant and projectible beyond the observed trajectory;
- sensitive to the system's actual history; and
- fixed before experiential attribution.

This is structure in a causal-modal sense: a fact about what a concrete system can and cannot physically do next. An abstract graph, extensional pattern, fitted probability distribution, computational equivalence, or isomorphism is insufficient.

The resulting structural position is compatible with Psillos's warning against treating structure as an empty shell wholly distinct from physical nature. Concrete causal roles and powers make the physical description substantive; they do not disclose ultimate categorical intrinsic constitution. Esfeld's causal-modal structuralism helps distinguish physical structure from merely mathematical structure. Ainsworth's analysis nevertheless requires every appeal to naturalness, modality, or privilege to receive an empirical interpretation rather than functioning as an unexplained label.

The result answers elementary Newman triviality **at schema level**. It is not
a completed scientific individuation procedure. The accepted investigation SOP
requires question-first, physically interpreted declarations, evidence, and
failure routes rather than a universal verification record.

## 7. The positive argument for identity

Physical individuation alone does not establish experiential identity. The strongest argument available is abductive and a posteriori:

1. Experience presents as one occurrent field of differentiated activity, not as a spectator plus an independently presented object.
2. The proposed qualifying UEF is hypothesized to match token unity, temporal extension, differentiation, change, boundaries, and cessation; complete matching in a concrete case remains to be demonstrated.
3. Complete physical determination leaves no autonomous psychophysical addition or independently variable experiential remainder.
4. Where constitution or realization is proposed as a distinct token relation, or experience is treated as an effect or correlate, IER asks for an independently specified experiential causal role, boundary, duration, or identity condition beyond the operation. The absence of such an account is an abductive consideration, not a deductive exclusion of those views.
5. Exact token coextension across all of these dimensions is best explained by numerical identity.

The provisional conclusion is:

```text
this token UEF operation
    = this total experience-as-process
    = this experiential subject
```

This is more than the inference that the process correlated with consciousness is consciousness. The physical operation must be independently specified, and the argument appeals to phenomenological unity, physical completeness, matched token structure, and ontological economy.

It remains defeasible. It does not derive what an experience feels like from third-person equations, prove that all phenomenological structure has been matched, or force acceptance by a critic who rejects the physicalist or anti-spectator premises. Identity may stop a demand for a second producer of experience without completing the qualitative correspondence programme.

| Candidate relation | Why numerical identity is preferred |
| --- | --- |
| Constitution | Where a distinct experiential token is proposed, its independent identity conditions require explanation; constitution views need not posit a second event |
| Realization | Type-level realization does not settle the selected token identity; sophisticated token accounts need separate engagement |
| Causal production | Makes experience an effect downstream from the qualifying operation |
| Correlation or isomorphism | Leaves two perfectly coextensive relata and does not explain shared token boundaries |
| Strong emergence | Adds a further fundamental fact or law contrary to complete physical determination |
| Numerical identity | Treats the physical and experiential descriptions as descriptions of one token |

## 8. Two clarifying contrasts

### Beni's structural self

Beni provides relevant prior art for an anti-substantival structural account of selfhood. His account unifies self-concepts through a generic information-processing pattern associated with cortical midline activity. It does not identify the total experiential bearer, require concrete history-sensitive nonfactorization, or supply a detailed persistence boundary. IER's claim is stronger and narrower: not that a self is a pattern, but that one concrete continuing operation is the experience and subject itself. A UEF does not require a narrative self, self-model, self-representation, or reflective awareness.

### Hershenov's intermittent organism

Hershenov permits an organism to exist again after a gap when its numerically identical final matter is restored in the relevant terminal arrangement. That proposal can be coherent only because the organism is not identified with the biological activity that ceases.

IER makes the opposite ontological choice. Matter, organism, configuration, stored information, and restart capacity are participants or conditions, not the process-subject bearer. When UEF operation is genuinely absent, the subject is absent; a later UEF is new. Exact memories or an apparent first-person sense of continuation do not bridge numerical identity across the gap.

## 9. What the research establishes

The completed programme supports the following claims.

1. A concrete process can be a numerically particular individual without a fixed material inventory.
2. The transition by which change is produced and borne is identity-relevant; resemblance and generic causal continuity are insufficient.
3. IER can formulate a wholly physical, nonexperiential process-persistence criterion without using consciousness as an input.
4. The criterion can allow turnover, boundary migration, nesting, and major internal change while rejecting branching identity and survival through cessation.
5. The full constitutive-membership relation and typed maximality block inclusion by causal ancestry alone; they do not supply a general boundary algorithm.
6. Pre-fixed, intervention-sensitive, history-bearing causal-modal relations answer the elementary Newman triviality objection at the level of a defensible schema.
7. IER avoids the constitutive-micropsychist combination problem by rejecting phenomenal composition, not by explaining how microsubjects fuse.
8. Complete physical determination does not entail experiential constituent inheritance.
9. The best positive case for UEF identity is a posteriori and abductive, appealing to proposed token coextension and the absence of an independently individuated experiential remainder; concrete matching remains to be demonstrated.
10. The specification connects nonfactorizable unity, owned successor production and typed inheritance, process-subject identity, and unique two-sided persistence. Phase 9/S5 finalizes a framework contribution claim; no component or conjunction is claimed as historically novel.

## 10. What remains unresolved

The following burdens should be stated openly in any paper.

### Worked physical instantiation

The theory still needs a worked instantiation of the full relation stack:
independently justified candidate, grain, restrictions, partition family,
ownership, membership, successor production, typed inheritance, and two-sided
uniqueness. Bounded S2 is an accepted physical subcase, not that instantiation.
A general non-gerrymandered empirical individuation procedure remains absent.

### Synchronic Unity and Concrete Attribution

Unity is intensionally specified by the exhaustive same-fibre nonfactorization
relation, with ownership and membership separately required. The remaining
burden is its physically justified operationalization and concrete attribution,
not invention of a further unity producer. Schema-level physical non-triviality
does not establish a particular UEF or a third-person certificate.

### Qualitative correspondence

IER still owes an account of how particular causal-modal organizations correspond to particular qualitative characters and phenomenal geometries. Rejecting microquality aggregation removes one bad derivation; it does not supply the positive mapping.

### Identity versus constitution and realization

The abductive identity argument needs fuller engagement with sophisticated constitution and realization views. Ontological economy and matched boundaries favor identity, but a critic can dispute whether the experiential description has genuinely been exhausted by the physical individuation.

### Empirical attribution and structural privacy

Ontological noncircularity does not yield an infallible third-person criterion for actual UEF presence. The project should distinguish: support for a physical model, success in individuating a causal-modal operation, and warrant for judging that operation experiential. Structural privacy limits certification only after the physical and metaphysical arguments are made; it cannot select the relations or compensate for weak evidence.

### Scope of the anti-panpsychist conclusion

The argument directly rejects constitutive micropsychism and experiential-ultimate inheritance. It does not automatically refute every Russellian, panprotopsychist, cosmopsychist, or nonconstitutive panpsychist view. These positions require separate engagement.

## External Dialectical Objection: Intrinsic Nature

Strawson's intrinsic nature concerns categorical or qualitative being; IER's
intrinsic constraint and intrinsic closure concern physically borne continuation
and its nonfactorization. They are not interchangeable.

IER's existing position is that reality-in-itself is mind-independent and
constraining, while its intrinsic constitution is unavailable to experiential
access. Constraint geometry describes relations without asserting that ultimate
constitution is exhausted or known. IER does not undertake to identify what
reality is ultimately made of.

Phenomenology concerns UEF-relative constraint organization, not the intrinsic
nature of an external cause. Sensory multiple realizability permits
different-cause/same-mode and same-cause/different-mode organization, blocking
an inverse inference from mode to external intrinsic nature. Coarse mode
equivalence is not complete phenomenal identity. Neither felt certainty nor
irreversible consequence provides a correctness signal or epistemic authority.

A Russellian critic can still demand a nonrelational experiential ground.
Failure to persuade that critic is an external dialectical disagreement,
not an unfinished internal IER mechanism. This classification does not
refute every Russellian position or discharge qualitative correspondence.
See `IER-reality.md`, `IER-sensory-multiple-realizability.md`, and
`IER-russellian-monism.md`. This external objection remains distinct from the internal burdens above.
