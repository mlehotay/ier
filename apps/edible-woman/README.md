# Edible Woman

*A non-canonical literary application of Informational Experiential Realism (IER)*

## Status

Status: draft  
Authority: non-canonical  

This directory collects the IER application materials for Margaret Atwood's
*The Edible Woman*. The materials are literary bridge and reader-support
documents. They do not modify IER canon, establish new IER ontology, define a
diagnostic category, or treat the novel as evidence for IER's truth.

If any document here conflicts with the canonical IER corpus, the canonical
source controls.

## Reading Order

### [The Critical Conversation Around *The Edible Woman*](./IER-edible-woman-literary-context.md)

A map of established literary criticism, live interpretive disputes, and
specialist extensions. It explains how literary consensus works and identifies
which parts of the IER analysis are inherited, reframed, or genuinely proposed.

### [Becoming Inedible](./IER-edible-woman.md)

The main literary bridge article. It reads Marian MacAlpin's trajectory through
refusal, situation authorship, food affordance change, behavioural substitution,
the woman-shaped cake, and the return of first-person narration.

### [How to Read "Becoming Inedible"](./IER-edible-woman-reader.md)

A non-specialist companion guide. It explains the article's IER vocabulary,
separates textual description from literary inference and IER redescription,
and gives readers a way to evaluate the behavioural-substitution hypothesis
without first accepting IER's ontology.

### [Claims Ledger](./edible-woman-claims-ledger.md)

The working evidence and argument ledger for the bridge article. It records
textual claims, interpretive claims, IER applications, confidence levels, and
excluded or corrected claims.

## Boundary

The collection is an application workspace under `apps/`. It is separate from
the remaining fiction examples under `examples/cultural-forms/fiction/`, which
continue to hold shorter reference examples and source materials.
