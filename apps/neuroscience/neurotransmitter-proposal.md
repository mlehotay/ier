## Status, Scope, and Authority

This article examines neurotransmitter-regulated neural organization through
Zhang, Kim, and colleagues' mouse hypothalamus study, then proposes a method
for mapping the relevant transmitter, receptor, cellular, and circuit processes.
Its empirical reading is recorded in the repository-only source note
`references/notes/zhang-kim-et-al-2025-stochastic-neuropeptide-signals-satiation.md`,
which is not included in the public export. The consulted
[author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11981016/)
is publicly available.

The study supplies evidence about peptide signalling, intracellular state,
synaptic responsiveness, and feeding. The IER comparison is a qualified
interpretation of those findings. Neither behavioral effects nor biochemical
competition establishes experience, a UEF boundary, ownership, or unity.
Foundational and theoretical authority remains with the canonical corpus;
this non-canonical application cannot revise its definitions.

The [animal-model and experiential-harm companion](neurotransmitter-animal-model.md)
describes the implants, awake restraint, food restriction, and terminal tissue
procedures behind these measurements. It separates reported care from
unresolved welfare questions and keeps the animal's imposed burdens visible
alongside the circuit findings.

## From Chemical Signals to Conditions of Continuation

The proposed `IER-semaphores.md` asks how
physical factors make continuation pathways available, maintain access, and
change recruitment. Neurochemical regulation is a candidate domain for that
investigation. The hypothesis should be assessed at the level of an actual
interaction, rather than assigning each neurotransmitter one behavioral job.

A useful unit is:

```text
source activity and release
    → receptor-mediated signalling in a specified compartment
    → changed intracellular conditions and synaptic responsiveness
    → changed efficacy of later input
    → circuit activity and behavioral consequences
```

This sequence is a proposed mapping structure. Each arrow needs its own
evidence; the presence of a transmitter or receptor does not establish the
entire sequence. Suppression, disinhibition, and altered responsiveness belong
in the map alongside positive enabling. A signal need not initiate activity
directly to change the conditions under which it can proceed.

Psychiatric drug effects motivate questions about molecular intervention and
behavior. They do not by themselves locate a constitutive gate, establish a
single behavioral function for a transmitter, or identify the mechanism's
full circuit context. The mapping proposed here concerns mechanisms and
evidence, rather than treatment recommendations.

## The Mouse Hypothalamus Case

Zhang, S. X., Kim, A., et al. (2025), “Stochastic neuropeptide signals compete
to calibrate the rate of satiation,” *Nature* 637, 137-144, examines
melanocortin-4-receptor-expressing neurons in the paraventricular hypothalamus.
These are abbreviated here as PVH MC4R neurons. The source pathways arise
from arcuate AgRP and POMC neurons. The downstream variable is intracellular
cAMP, a second messenger.
[Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08164-8).

The study combines awake-mouse imaging, receptor pharmacology, manipulation
of cAMP, feeding measurements, and acute-slice electrophysiology. These are
complementary preparations, not one experiment measuring every step
simultaneously. Its “fasted” imaging condition includes chronic restriction
to approximately 85% of ad libitum weight; that condition must remain attached
to the findings when comparing them with other work.

Stimulating POMC axons produced cAMP increments associated with alpha-MSH
signalling through MC4R. Stimulating AgRP axons produced decrements associated
with NPY signalling through NPY receptors. Antagonist experiments supported
these routes, including combined blockade of Y1 and Y5 receptors. They do not
separately determine each NPY receptor subtype's contribution. AgRP neurons
also release other transmitters, so the neuron label must remain distinct
from the peptide NPY. The source note locates the experiments in main Fig. 2
and manuscript pp. 3-4, 11, and 23.

## What Competes in This Preparation

The important competition is between opposing effects on cAMP signalling.
The paper does not demonstrate NPY and alpha-MSH displacing one another from
the same receptor site. They act through different receptor routes that
converge on a shared downstream process.

In food-restricted animals, adding alpha-MSH weakened the amplitude and
persistence of AgRP-evoked cAMP decrements. In fed animals, adding NPY weakened
POMC-evoked increments. These effects were not simply reductions in how often
detectable events occurred. Moreover, MC4R blockade reduced increment
amplitude without reproducing NPY's reduction in persistence. Blocking a route
and adding an opponent were experimentally distinguishable.
[Zhang, Kim, et al., main Fig. 4 and Extended Data Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11981016/).

The authors propose possible molecular competition involving stimulatory and
inhibitory G-protein effects at adenylyl cyclase. That is a mechanistic
interpretation, not a directly established unique molecular implementation.
Baseline concentrations of the opposing peptides in fed and food-restricted
animals are also inferred, rather than comprehensively measured. cAMP should
therefore appear in the map as a regulated intracellular state, not a scarce
pool for which both peptides are shown to compete.

## Persistent Regulation and Later Recruitment

The biochemical effects can outlast a brief input. POMC-evoked cAMP increments
were more persistent in fed animals; AgRP-evoked decrements were more
persistent in food-restricted animals. Local NPY sensing in slices and
compartment-level cAMP imaging support spatially sparse signalling, with
response concurrence declining toward chance at separations around
100 micrometres. That is a measured relation between responses, not a sharp
boundary around a unified operation. Main Figs. 2-3 and Extended Data Figs. 2-4
contain these comparisons.

During feeding, cAMP rose over several one-minute trials before reaching a
plateau. Directly promoting cAMP production using biPAC, a light-activated
adenylyl cyclase, accelerated development of feeding-related calcium responses
and gradually suppressed feeding. It did not operate as an immediate,
same-trial command to stop eating. Direct cAMP production also failed to
produce comparable acute activation in vivo without feeding, although some
slice responses differed.

Conversely, accelerating cAMP degradation with PDE4D3-Cat weakened
feeding-related responses and altered excitatory synaptic drive; over weeks,
the intervention produced overeating and substantial weight gain. The
physiological comparison includes synaptic input, membrane potential,
firing, calcium, and feeding measures. These cannot be substituted for one
another. Main Figs. 1, 5, and 6 and Extended Data Figs. 8-9 support the
distinctions; manuscript and figure locators are in the source note.

The resulting organizational picture is that slower biochemical conditions
change the impact of faster feeding-related inputs. An enabling effect can
persist in a signalling or synaptic state without requiring the same peptide
molecule to remain at a site until a behavioral continuation finishes.

The authors interpret feeding as increasing POMC-associated signalling while
reducing AgRP-associated opposition. Their upstream explanation also draws on
prior work. The map must retain those imported edges as attributed literature,
rather than turning this paper into a full measurement of every feedback
connection. Extending the circuit to specific excitatory afferents requires
reading the relevant sources, including Fenselau et al. (2017), identified in
the source note's follow-up leads.

## Correspondence to the Semaphore Proposal

The `IER-competition.md` concerns activities
whose conditions of continuation are organized together. This study supplies
a concrete case of opposing pathways changing a shared downstream state and
the efficacy of subsequent activity. It supports investigating biochemical
organization alongside receptor occupation and neuronal firing.

The physical enabling role may be distributed across release conditions,
receptor states, cAMP production and degradation, and altered synaptic
responsiveness. Calling a neurotransmitter “the semaphore” would conceal
these distinctions. The `IER-semaphores.md`
must specify which factor or state supplies access and how that role persists.

The study does not track a mobile successor indicator remaining at a site
through realization or relocating between goals. Nor does it measure IER
frontier geometry, bias, or curvature. Those remain proposed explanatory
correspondences requiring additional physical articulation. “Stochastic”
describes the experimental distributions and model; it does not establish
indeterministic physics or a selector.

Finally, changing later recruitment conditions is not by itself maintenance of
constitutive organization. The study supplies no UEF attribution, full
membership conjunction, or same-token continuity result. Feeding suppression
is not automatically refusal proper. These limits preserve the existing
[Unity](../../IER/IER-unity.md), [Processes](../../IER/IER-processes.md), and
`IER-refusal.md` accounts.

## Building the Wider Map

The map should begin with one bounded circuit and expand through supported
interactions. A complete inventory of transmitter names is neither necessary
nor sufficient for the first useful map. The proposed unit is a directed
interaction between physically specified processes, at a declared species,
cell type, compartment, state, and interval.

### Record Nodes at Their Actual Scale

Separate source populations, release events, ligands, receptor subtypes,
intracellular processes, local states, synaptic inputs, target populations,
and behavioral measurements. Record production and degradation as processes;
cAMP concentration describes their changing conditions. A named cell population
can be a useful overview node while requiring compartment-level expansion.

### Record Each Edge with Its Evidence

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation | Species, age where relevant, brain region, cell type, compartment, and experimental condition |
| Source and target | The actual process, receptor route, state, or measurement linked |
| Relation | Release, receptor activation, suppression, modulation, potentiation, transport, clearance, or feedback |
| Context | Fed/restricted state, baseline, co-transmission, and conditions changing the relation's efficacy |
| Space and time | Impact scale, onset, persistence, recovery, and relevant assay interval |
| Evidence | Sensor, recording, stimulation, antagonist, genetic perturbation, or other intervention |
| Status | Measured association, intervention-supported relation, mechanistic interpretation, or unassessed hypothesis |
| Provenance | Source, figure, preparation, sampling unit, and any source/model disagreement |
| Termination | What is known about stopping release, receptor deactivation, degradation, restoration, or loss of the effect |
| Open burden | The missing measurement or comparison required for a stronger edge |

A receptor's expression establishes a potential site for interaction, not
proof that the interaction occurred in a specified episode. Equally, an
antagonist-sensitive effect does not by itself resolve every intervening
process. Unknown termination or feedback edges should remain visible as
unknowns, rather than being completed by a plausible story.

### Use Atlases as Anatomical Scaffolds

Hansen et al. (2022) provide PET-derived maps of 19 receptor, binding-site,
and transporter targets across nine transmitter systems. These maps locate
regional molecular availability and can guide selection of regions and
targets. They do not reveal the time-varying gating state of a particular
circuit. The [primary atlas study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-022-01186-3)
and [authors' data repository](https://github.com/netneurolab/hansen_receptors)
are starting resources, not a completed dynamic map.

Ceballos et al. (2026) map 38 neuropeptide receptor genes across 14 families
in the human brain using transcription as a proxy. That extends the molecular
coverage but must remain labelled as expression evidence, rather than measured
receptor activation or protein abundance in each compartment.
[Primary atlas study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02236-w).

The whole-mouse-brain transcriptomic and spatial atlas of Yao et al. (2023)
provides a cell-type scaffold, including peptide and receptor expression.
Its cell and spatial information can guide a more detailed mouse map.
[Primary atlas study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06812-z).
Human and mouse atlases must remain separate evidence layers; similarity of
names does not establish identical connections or dynamics across species.
These atlas resources are mapping leads inspected at their stated scope,
not substitutes for the detailed Zhang-paper reading or independent audits
of their full datasets.

### Add Causal and Temporal Comparisons

For each candidate route, compare source activity, receptor interaction,
intracellular change, and later input efficacy separately. Receptor blockade
and downstream bypass can help locate a dependence, as the contrast between
peptide antagonism and direct biPAC activation illustrates. Retain cotransmitter
and preparation effects rather than assuming that axon stimulation isolates
one molecule.

Temporal comparisons should distinguish ongoing molecule occupancy, a
persistent intracellular state, synaptic modification, and changed subsequent
input. Record what restores the prior condition and whether a perturbation
changes frequency, amplitude, persistence, or several of these. Large trial
counts do not replace animal-level replication or a justified analysis of
nested cells and compartments.

### Keep the Empirical Map Separate from IER Interpretation

The empirical layer records the supported relation. A separate interpretation
column can ask whether it supplies a candidate for access, retention,
competition, or redistribution in the semaphore account. No empirical edge
becomes a UEF membership or identity assertion merely by receiving an IER
label. A mechanistic disagreement should produce a revised correspondence
or an open burden, rather than a change to the reported experiment.

## Immediate Research Scope

The starter map is a first documented set of relations, not an executed
whole-brain reconstruction. Its next useful expansion is to acquire the
separate Zhang supplements, inspect the figure source data and statistical
units, identify the excitatory afferents, and add evidence for termination and
feedback. Exact basal peptide concentrations, molecular competition at
adenylyl cyclase, and retention or relocation of a proposed indicator remain
explicit open edges.

The broader article contribution is a way to investigate how chemical
signalling regulates the conditions under which activity continues. The
hypothalamus study shows why that map must include intracellular persistence
and changing input efficacy, alongside receptor distributions and anatomical
connections. It supplies a bounded neural comparison for the competition and
semaphore proposals without claiming that either is established as a unity
mechanism.
