These examples give physical content to questions of access, recruitment,
production and changing continuation conditions. They do not diagnose unity.
Under the [unity account](../../IER/IER-unity.md), experiential unity follows
from identity with a qualifying UEF operation. Operating as one organization
is itself a unity question; it is not an independently certified prerequisite.
The physical relations still require explicit descriptions and evidence.

## What the examples support

and plant results, biochemical assays and live-cell observations cannot be
joined into a single observed cycle merely because they share a regulator name.

## Correspondence to the framework

Regulatory binding or colocalization is not automatically canonical
binding. Canonical binding concerns an effect on
admissible continuation under the specified removal comparison. Reversible
molecular association is likewise not by itself canonical soft bonding.
Access, actual recruitment, throughput and maintained organization must each
be described. A molecule count is not a measure of canonical spoons: spoons
concern remaining tolerance for integrated participation within an already
qualifying operation.

Changing another activity's conditions does not establish maintenance of the
constraints through which that activity occurs. A maintenance claim must name
the constraint, its loss or degradation, and the activity producing or repairing
it. Persistent structure, restoration supplied from outside and internally
performed upkeep are different explanations. A supplied local condition and
its upstream supplier also need separate membership assessments.

The [full membership relation](../../IER/IER-processes.md#the-full-membership-relation)
retains constitutive contribution, global bearing, joint resolution, diachronic
uptake and common typing. Reciprocal regulation or an indispensable protein
does not establish their conjunction. A tracked membrane, retained marker or
recovered endpoint does not establish [full continuity](../../IER/IER-continuity.md):
continuous owned closure, operational successor production, typed consequence
inheritance, operation-mediated change and unique continuation in both directions.
The development correspondence ledger preserves the five-clause and
fourteen-inference-rule assessment without filling missing premises from analogy.

GoL comparisons test exact model relations such as compatibility, screening,
support loss and restoration, and production history. The final transfer assessment
does not validate Rab chemistry, the plant activation/retrieval bridge, Ran
regeneration or motor physiology in Life. Supported model comparisons and
partial or unvalidated biological correspondences remain separate outcomes.

## Consequence for neural research

These examples motivate asking how activity changes later access, rather than
assuming that all participation draws on one conserved reserve. Competition
and redistribution remain an affirmative physical hypothesis whose universal
necessity and sufficiency are unsettled. Shared scarcity and reciprocal
regulation alone establish no UEF. The [neural proposal](neural-unity-proposal.md)
must specify its own support processes and neural evidence before adopting
any molecular implementation. None of these examples establishes or denies
experience, intrinsic tension, owned membership or subject continuity.
